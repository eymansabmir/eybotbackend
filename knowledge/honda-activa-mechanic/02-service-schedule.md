# Honda Activa 6G — Service Schedule

## First Service

- Interval: 750–1000 km
- Period: 30 days
- Activities:
  - Engine oil replacement
  - General inspection
  - Brake inspection

## Second Service

- Interval: 5500–6000 km
- Period: 6 months
- Activities:
  - Engine oil replacement
  - Air filter cleaning
  - Brake adjustment

## Third Service

- Interval: 11500–12000 km
- Period: 12 months
- Activities:
  - Oil change
  - Air filter inspection
  - Spark plug inspection
  - CVT inspection

## Recurring Service

- Frequency: Every 6000 km **or** 6 months (whichever comes first)
