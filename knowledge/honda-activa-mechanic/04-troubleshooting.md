# Honda Activa 6G — Troubleshooting

## Vehicle Not Starting

### Symptoms

- Self start not working
- Kick start difficult
- Engine cranks but does not start

### Potential Causes

- Dead battery
- Blown fuse
- Faulty starter relay
- Faulty ignition switch
- Loose wiring
- Dirty spark plug
- Fuel injector issue
- No fuel
- Low engine compression

### Diagnostic Flow

1. Check battery voltage
2. Check fuse
3. Check spark plug
4. Verify fuel supply
5. Verify spark
6. Check compression
7. Check valve clearance

## Poor Pickup

### Potential Causes

- Dirty air filter
- Worn CVT belt
- Worn rollers
- Carbon deposits
- Fuel injector blockage
- Low compression

### Recommended Actions

- Check air filter
- Inspect CVT belt
- Inspect rollers
- Clean throttle body
- Inspect injector
- Perform compression test

## Low Mileage

### Potential Causes

- Dirty air filter
- Incorrect tyre pressure
- Fuel injector issue
- Worn spark plug
- Poor fuel quality

### Recommended Actions

- Replace air filter
- Check tyre pressure
- Clean injector
- Replace spark plug
- Use recommended fuel
