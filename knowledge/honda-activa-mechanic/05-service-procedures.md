# Honda Activa 6G — Service Procedures

## Battery Replacement

1. Open seat and battery cover
2. Disconnect negative terminal
3. Disconnect positive terminal
4. Remove battery
5. Install new battery
6. Connect positive terminal
7. Connect negative terminal
8. Verify charging voltage

## CVT Belt Replacement

1. Remove CVT cover
2. Inspect rollers
3. Remove worn belt
4. Inspect clutch assembly
5. Install new belt
6. Reassemble CVT cover
7. Perform road test

## Brake Shoe Replacement

1. Remove wheel
2. Open drum assembly
3. Inspect brake shoe wear
4. Replace worn shoes
5. Reassemble drum
6. Adjust brake free play
7. Test braking performance
