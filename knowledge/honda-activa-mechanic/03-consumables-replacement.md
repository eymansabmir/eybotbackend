# Honda Activa 6G — Consumables & Replacement Intervals

## Engine Oil

- Grade: SAE 10W30
- Capacity: 800 ml
- Replace: Every 6000 km

## Air Filter

- Clean: Every 4000 km
- Replace: Every 12000 km

## Spark Plug

- Inspect: Every service
- Replace: 12000 km

## CVT Belt

- Inspect: 6000–8000 km
- Replace: 20000–24000 km

## Brake Shoes

- Inspect: Every service
- Replace: On wear

## Battery

- Expected life: 2–3 years

## Gear Oil

- Replace: 6000–12000 km
