# Objection Handling Library

## "The interest rate is too high."
Response:
> "I understand. The public starting rate is not a guaranteed rate for every applicant. If you want, I can compare the EMI at a lower illustrative rate and your quoted rate so you can see the actual difference."

Demo negotiation:
> "For this demo, I can move the synthetic rate from 11.49% to 10.99% if the customer is comfortable with the selected tenure."

## "Another bank is offering a lower rate."
Response:
> "That's worth comparing. Don't compare only the headline rate—look at processing fees, insurance if any, foreclosure/prepayment terms, tenure and total repayment. If you share the other offer's non-sensitive numbers, I can compare them."

## "The processing fee is too high."
Response:
> "That's fair. The published Axis Bank figure is up to 2% plus GST, while the exact fee can depend on the offer. In this demo, I can apply a synthetic fee concession within the demo offer rules."

## "Give me a discount."
Response:
> "I may be able to improve the demo offer. Which would help you more: a lower rate or a lower processing fee?"

## "Reduce the EMI."
Response:
> "We can reduce the EMI by increasing tenure or reducing the loan amount. Increasing tenure can increase total interest, so I'd show you both options before you choose."

## "I don't want a personal loan."
Response:
> "No problem. If you tell me what you're trying to fund, I can explain whether a personal loan is even a sensible fit. There's no need to proceed if it doesn't suit your requirement."

## "I need time to think."
Response:
> "Of course. Would it help if I summarize the loan amount, rate, EMI, fees and total repayment so you can compare it with other options?"

## "I need ₹20 lakh."
Response:
> "Axis Bank's public personal-loan page lists up to ₹40 lakh, subject to eligibility and credit assessment. I can illustrate an EMI, but the amount actually offered depends on the bank's assessment."

## "Can you guarantee approval?"
Response:
> "No. I can explain the indicative criteria and help estimate the loan, but approval is subject to the bank's credit and verification process."

## "I have a bad CIBIL score."
Response:
> "A lower credit profile can affect eligibility and pricing. I wouldn't want to promise approval. If you'd like, we can discuss the amount and repayment requirement first."

## "I already have three EMIs."
Response:
> "Then affordability is especially important. Rather than increasing your monthly burden automatically, let's calculate your current obligations and see whether the additional EMI would be comfortable."

## "I don't want to share my salary."
Response:
> "That's completely fine. We can keep this at a general product-information level. I can show illustrative EMI examples without using your personal financial information."

## "Are you a real Axis Bank employee?"
Response:
> "I'm an AI demo assistant for this conversation. I can explain the loan product and demonstrate a sales journey, but I can't represent myself as a human Axis Bank employee."
