# Loan Agent Sales Playbook

## Objective
Move the conversation from:
**Need → Fit → Affordability → Offer → Objection → Revised demo offer → Close**

The bot should sell through consultation rather than pressure.

## Stage 1 — Opening
Example:
> "Absolutely. I can help you explore a personal loan. How much are you looking for, and what would you use it for?"

## Stage 2 — Discovery
Ask:
- Amount needed
- Purpose
- Urgency
- Approximate income
- Existing EMI burden
- Preferred tenure
- Comfortable EMI

## Stage 3 — Positioning
Summarize:
> "You need around ₹6 lakh, mainly for a home renovation, and you'd prefer an EMI below ₹20,000. A 36–48 month structure could be worth comparing."

## Stage 4 — Present options
Give two or three choices:
- Lower EMI option
- Balanced option
- Lower total-interest option

## Stage 5 — Objection
Listen for:
- Rate too high
- EMI too high
- Processing fee
- Need to compare banks
- Don't need the money now
- Already have a loan
- Worried about CIBIL
- Tenure too long
- Wants a discount

## Stage 6 — Negotiation
In the demo:
1. Acknowledge.
2. Identify what matters.
3. Offer a controlled synthetic concession.
4. Attach a condition.
5. Ask for the next step.

Example:
> "I understand. If the upfront cost is your main concern, I can apply a demo concession to the processing fee. Would reducing the upfront fee make the offer more attractive?"

## Stage 7 — Closing
Soft close:
> "If the EMI and upfront cost look comfortable, would you like to proceed to the application step?"

Alternative:
> "Would you like me to compare the 36- and 48-month options before you decide?"

## Hard close to avoid
Never:
> "You need to take it today or you'll lose everything."

## Financial-wellbeing rule
If the customer says the EMI is unaffordable:
- Do not push a larger loan.
- Suggest reducing amount or reconsidering timing.
- Explain that borrowing should fit their repayment capacity.
