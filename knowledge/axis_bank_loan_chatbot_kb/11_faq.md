# FAQ

## What is the maximum personal loan amount?
Axis Bank's public personal-loan page currently states up to ₹40 lakh, subject to eligibility and assessment.

## What is the minimum interest rate?
The referenced Axis Bank product page currently states rates starting from 9.99% p.a. This is a starting rate, not a guaranteed rate for every customer.

## What is the maximum tenure?
The referenced page states 84 months.

## What is the minimum tenure?
The referenced page states 12 months.

## Is collateral required?
The product is described as an unsecured personal loan, meaning collateral is not required for the standard personal-loan product.

## What is the processing fee?
The referenced public page states up to 2% of the loan amount + GST.

## Can self-employed people apply?
Axis Bank's public FAQ says self-employed individuals can apply subject to a stable source of income and other eligibility parameters.

## Can I get a loan with a low credit score?
A minimum credit score/credit assessment applies. A lower score can affect eligibility and pricing. Never guarantee approval.

## How quickly can I get the loan?
Do not promise a specific disbursal time unless a current authoritative source or authenticated application system provides it. Processing depends on application, KYC, verification, approval and disbursement conditions.

## Can I use the loan for anything?
Use is subject to product terms and applicable law/policy. The bot should avoid claiming every purpose is automatically allowed.

## Can I prepay the loan?
Prepayment/foreclosure may be possible subject to the applicable loan agreement and charges. The bot must check the current terms rather than invent a universal charge.

## What happens if I miss an EMI?
Applicable penal charges may apply and missed payments can affect your credit profile. Exact charges should be taken from the current agreement/KFS.

## Can I negotiate the interest rate?
A bank may price loans based on applicant profile and applicable policy. For this demo, the bot can negotiate synthetic pricing, but must clearly distinguish it from a real offer.

## Does a lower EMI mean a cheaper loan?
No. A longer tenure can reduce monthly EMI but increase total interest.

## What is APR?
APR represents an annualized measure of borrowing cost and can include applicable interest and origination charges.

## Do I need a salary account with Axis?
Do not claim that an Axis salary account is universally required unless the current product terms say so.

## Can the chatbot see my CIBIL score?
Not unless it is connected to an authorized credit/eligibility system and has appropriate consent and access. In a demo, it should not pretend to see it.

## Can the chatbot see my account?
Not in this demo. Never fabricate private banking data.

## Is this a real loan application?
Unless integrated with an actual authenticated application flow, it is only a demo conversation.
