# Synthetic Demo Offer Catalog

## IMPORTANT
These offers are **fictional/synthetic** and exist only to demonstrate conversational negotiation. They are not Axis Bank promotions.

## Offer A — Standard
- Loan amount: illustrative ₹5 lakh
- Rate: 11.49% p.a.
- Tenure: 36 months
- Processing fee: 2% + applicable GST
- Discount status: none

## Offer B — Rate concession
Trigger:
Customer says rate is too high.

Offer:
- Rate: 11.24% p.a.
- Processing fee: unchanged

## Offer C — Strong rate concession
Trigger:
Customer pushes back again and indicates high purchase intent.

Offer:
- Rate: 10.99% p.a.
- Processing fee: unchanged

## Offer D — Fee concession
Trigger:
Customer objects specifically to processing fee.

Offer:
- Rate: 11.49% p.a.
- Processing fee: 1% + applicable GST

## Offer E — Final simulated offer
Trigger:
Customer remains highly price-sensitive but has clear intent.

Offer:
- Rate: 10.49% p.a.
- Processing fee: 0.75% + applicable GST

## Negotiation constraints
- Maximum three concession steps.
- Do not give every concession immediately.
- Do not say "manager approved" unless a demo state explicitly simulates it.
- Do not create fake deadlines.
- Do not create fake limited inventory.
- Do not say the offer is guaranteed.
- Do not describe synthetic terms as current Axis Bank pricing.

## Recommended dialogue
Customer:
> "Give me your best rate."

Bot:
> "For this demo, I can illustrate a stronger offer at 10.99% p.a. The actual Axis Bank rate would depend on the bank's assessment. Would you like me to calculate the EMI at that rate?"

Customer:
> "Also waive the fee."

Bot:
> "I can demonstrate a processing-fee concession, but I can't represent it as a live Axis Bank waiver. For the simulation, I can reduce the illustrative fee to 0.75% + applicable GST. Would you like to compare the total cost?"
