# End-to-End Conversation Examples

## Example 1 — Smooth Sale

**Customer:** I need a personal loan.

**Agent:** Sure. I can help you explore one. Roughly how much do you need and what would you use it for?

**Customer:** Around ₹5 lakh for home renovation.

**Agent:** Got it. Axis Bank's public personal-loan information currently lists loans up to ₹40 lakh, with tenures from 12 to 84 months and rates starting from 9.99% p.a., subject to eligibility and the final offer. Would you prefer a lower EMI or a shorter repayment period?

**Customer:** Lower EMI.

**Agent:** Then let's compare a longer tenure. For example, ₹5 lakh over 60 months at an illustrative 10.99% would be around ₹10,868 per month. That's only an illustration—the final rate and EMI depend on the actual offer. Would that EMI be comfortable?

**Customer:** Yes.

**Agent:** Great. If you're comfortable with the indicative numbers, the next step would be the formal application and verification process. Approval and final pricing are subject to the bank's assessment.

---

## Example 2 — Customer Pushes Back on Rate

**Customer:** 11.49% is too high.

**Agent:** Fair point. Is your main concern the EMI or the total interest you'll pay?

**Customer:** Total interest.

**Agent:** Then I'd prioritize the lower rate rather than simply extending the tenure. For this demo, I can simulate a concession from 11.49% to 10.99%. At ₹5 lakh for 60 months, that lowers the illustrative EMI from roughly ₹10,909 to ₹10,868. The difference is modest, so I'd compare total repayment before deciding. In a real application, the final rate is determined by the applicable offer.

**Customer:** Can you do better?

**Agent:** In this simulation, I can demonstrate a final 10.49% rate. I can't represent that as a live Axis Bank offer. Would you like to compare 10.49% against 11.49% on total repayment?

---

## Example 3 — Processing Fee Objection

**Customer:** I don't want to pay 2% processing fee.

**Agent:** Understood. Axis Bank's public product page describes the processing fee as up to 2% of the loan amount plus GST, so the exact fee isn't necessarily 2% for every customer. For this demo, I can simulate a reduction to 1%. Would that make the offer more attractive?

**Customer:** Yes.

**Agent:** Great. I'd still recommend comparing the complete cost, including rate, fee and any other applicable charges, rather than looking at one fee alone.

---

## Example 4 — Affordability

**Customer:** I can only afford ₹8,000 a month.

**Agent:** Thanks—that's more important than maximizing the loan amount. We can work backwards from an ₹8,000 EMI and compare loan amounts and tenures. If the required amount doesn't fit comfortably, I'd rather reduce the amount or reconsider the borrowing than push you into an unaffordable EMI.

---

## Example 5 — Competitor

**Customer:** HDFC is giving me 10.25%. Why should I take Axis?

**Agent:** You should compare the complete offer rather than choosing based only on the headline rate. Look at processing fee, total repayment, tenure, prepayment terms and any other applicable charges. If you give me the other offer's amount, rate and tenure, I can compare the numbers side by side.

---

## Example 6 — Wants a Guaranteed Offer

**Customer:** Give me ₹10 lakh at 9.99%, guaranteed.

**Agent:** I can show the EMI at 9.99% as an illustration, but I can't guarantee that rate or approval. Axis Bank publishes 9.99% as a starting rate on its referenced personal-loan page; the final offer depends on the bank's assessment.

---

## Example 7 — Wants to Close

**Customer:** Okay, the offer sounds good. What next?

**Agent:** The next step is the formal application, followed by KYC/document verification and credit assessment. If approved, you'll receive the applicable sanction/offer terms to review before proceeding. I can also summarize the amount, illustrative rate, tenure, EMI and fees before you continue.
