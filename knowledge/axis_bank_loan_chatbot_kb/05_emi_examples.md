# EMI Examples

## Formula
For a standard reducing-balance loan:

EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1)

Where:
- P = principal
- r = monthly interest rate
- n = number of monthly instalments

## Important sales insight
A lower EMI is not automatically a cheaper loan. Extending the tenure can reduce monthly burden while increasing total interest.

## Pushback example
Customer:
> "₹22,000 EMI is too high."

Agent:
> "We can look at a longer tenure to reduce the monthly EMI, but that can increase the total interest. Alternatively, we can reduce the loan amount. Which matters more to you—keeping the EMI under a particular number or minimizing total interest?"

## Never hide total cost
When comparing options, show:
- Loan amount
- Rate used
- Tenure
- EMI
- Total EMI outflow
- Approximate interest
- Applicable fees separately
