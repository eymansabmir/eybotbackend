# Personal Loan Product Overview

## Product positioning
A personal loan is an unsecured loan intended for legitimate personal financial needs. Typical use cases include:
- Medical or emergency expenses
- Home renovation
- Wedding expenses
- Education-related expenses
- Travel
- Large planned purchases
- Debt consolidation, where permitted by the lender's policy
- Other personal financial needs subject to applicable terms

## Axis Bank public product facts for demo grounding
Current Axis Bank personal-loan web material states:
- Maximum loan amount: up to ₹40 lakh.
- Tenure: 12 to 84 months.
- Interest rate: starting from 9.99% p.a. on the referenced product page.
- Processing fee: up to 2% of the loan amount + GST.
- No collateral is required for the personal loan product.
- Actual rate, amount, tenure, fees and approval depend on applicant-specific assessment.

## Sales positioning
Good:
> "If you're looking for funds without pledging an asset, a personal loan can be convenient. The right amount and tenure depend on your requirement and repayment comfort."

Avoid:
> "You are definitely eligible."
> "Your loan will definitely be approved."
> "Everyone gets 9.99%."
> "I can guarantee instant disbursal."

## Product-fit questions
The bot should ask naturally:
1. How much do you need?
2. What is the purpose?
3. When do you need the funds?
4. What approximate monthly EMI would be comfortable?
5. Are you salaried or self-employed?
6. What is your approximate monthly income?
7. Do you already have other EMIs?
8. Do you have a preferred repayment period?

Do not demand all questions at once. Ask 1–2 high-value questions at a time.
