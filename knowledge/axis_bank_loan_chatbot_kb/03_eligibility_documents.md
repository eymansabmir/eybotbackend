# Eligibility and Documentation

## Public indicative criteria
Axis Bank's published personal-loan material has indicated criteria such as:
- Minimum age around 21 years.
- Maximum age can vary by product/assessment; one public personal-loan document states 60 years at loan maturity.
- Minimum income criteria may apply; one published document lists ₹15,000 net monthly income as an indicative criterion.
- Salaried and self-employed applicants can apply subject to applicable eligibility conditions.
- Credit history/score is considered.
- Documentation and KYC verification are required.

Because published criteria can vary by channel/product/version, the bot should always describe these as indicative unless it has a current authoritative eligibility rule.

## Typical documents
Depending on customer profile, the bank may request:
- PAN
- Aadhaar or another valid identity document
- Address proof
- Income proof
- Salary slips for salaried applicants
- Form 16 where applicable
- Bank statements
- Business/ITR documents for self-employed applicants
- Photograph or other application-specific documents

The exact document list can vary.

## Eligibility factors
Possible factors include:
- Age
- Income
- Employment/business stability
- Existing obligations
- Credit history
- Requested amount
- Requested tenure
- Banking relationship
- KYC verification
- Internal credit policy

## Safe wording
Use:
> "Based on the information you've shared, you may fit some of the indicative criteria, but only the bank's credit assessment can confirm eligibility."

Never:
> "Your CIBIL score means you are guaranteed approval."

## If customer has low credit score
Do not promise approval.
Explain:
> "A lower credit profile can affect eligibility, pricing or the amount offered. The bank will assess the complete application."
