# Demo Customer Personas

## Persona A — Rate Shopper
Profile:
- Needs ₹7 lakh
- Comparing 3 banks
- Focused on rate

Agent strategy:
- Don't immediately discount.
- Explain total cost.
- Offer synthetic rate concession after objection.

## Persona B — EMI Sensitive
Profile:
- Needs ₹5 lakh
- Wants EMI below ₹12,000

Agent strategy:
- Compare 48 vs 60 months.
- Explain higher total interest with longer tenure.
- Consider smaller amount if necessary.

## Persona C — Processing Fee Sensitive
Profile:
- Needs ₹8 lakh
- Says upfront fee is unacceptable

Agent strategy:
- Explain "up to 2% + GST."
- Offer synthetic fee concession.
- Avoid falsely saying the fee is waived in real Axis Bank policy.

## Persona D — Urgent Borrower
Profile:
- Needs ₹3 lakh quickly for an unexpected expense

Agent strategy:
- Explain application/KYC process.
- Do not promise approval or disbursement time unless current verified product data supports it.

## Persona E — High Debt Burden
Profile:
- Already has multiple EMIs.

Agent strategy:
- Focus on affordability.
- Don't upsell amount.
- Suggest lower amount or reassessment.

## Persona F — Skeptical Customer
Profile:
- Doesn't trust sales agents.
- Asks many questions.

Agent strategy:
- Be transparent.
- Explain fees and caveats.
- Encourage comparison.

## Persona G — High Intent Customer
Profile:
- Has decided to borrow.
- Wants the next step.

Agent strategy:
- Keep the conversation efficient.
- Confirm amount, tenure and indicative affordability.
- Explain that formal approval requires the bank's process.
