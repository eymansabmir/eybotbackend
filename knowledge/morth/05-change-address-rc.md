# Change Address on RC

## User questions

- Change address on my RC
- Update address in RC
- My vehicle address has changed
- Change address on registration certificate
- RC address change online
- I moved to another city, how do I update my RC?

## Answer

Apply for **Change of Address in RC** through VAHAN Citizen Services.

## Portal

Vahan — https://vahan.parivahan.gov.in/vahanservice/vahan/

## Steps

1. Visit VAHAN Citizen Services.
2. Select **Vehicle Related Services**.
3. Enter vehicle registration number.
4. Enter required chassis details.
5. Generate and verify OTP.
6. Select **Change of Address in RC**.
7. Enter new address.
8. Update insurance details if requested.
9. Upload supporting documents.
10. Pay the applicable fee.
11. Book an appointment if required.
12. Complete RTO verification if required.

## Forms

Form 33 — intimation of change of address to be recorded in the RC.
