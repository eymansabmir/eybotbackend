# Hypothecation Termination (Loan Closed)

## User questions

- I finished my car loan
- Remove bank name from RC
- Remove hypothecation from RC
- Loan is closed, how do I update RC?
- Bank hypothecation removal
- Remove financier from RC

## Answer

After completing the vehicle loan, apply for **Termination of Hypothecation** through VAHAN.

## Portal

Vahan — https://vahan.parivahan.gov.in/vahanservice/vahan/

## Steps

1. Open VAHAN Citizen Services.
2. Enter vehicle registration details.
3. Select **Termination of Hypothecation** under vehicle services.
4. Complete application and upload documents from financier/RTO.
5. Pay applicable fee.
6. Complete verification or appointment if required.

## Notes

Exact documents and process depend on the financier and registering RTO.
