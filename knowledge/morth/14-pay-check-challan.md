# Pay / Check Traffic Challan

## User questions

- Check my challan
- Do I have any traffic challan?
- Pay traffic challan
- Challan status
- Vehicle challan
- How much is my challan?
- I want to pay my challan

## Answer

Check and pay traffic challans using the official **eChallan** service.

## Portal

eChallan — https://echallan.parivahan.gov.in/challan/challan-services

## Steps

1. Open the eChallan portal.
2. Select **Check Pending Challan Status**.
3. Search using vehicle number, challan number, or driving licence number.
4. Review pending challans.
5. Select the challan to pay.
6. Complete payment.
7. Download or print the payment receipt.

## If payment succeeded but no receipt

- Check **Pending Transaction**
- Download **Payment Receipt**
- Raise **Grievance** through the eChallan portal

## Notes

Provide only the vehicle, challan, or DL information required by the official service.
