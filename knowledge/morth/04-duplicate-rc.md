# Lost RC / Duplicate Registration Certificate

## User questions

- I lost my RC
- My RC is lost
- I lost my vehicle registration certificate
- How do I get a duplicate RC?
- RC stolen
- Duplicate RC online
- What should I do if my RC is lost?

## Answer

If the Registration Certificate is lost, stolen, damaged, or destroyed, apply for a **Duplicate RC** on VAHAN.

If lost or stolen, report to police and inform the registering authority. Duplicate RC application uses **Form 26**.

## Portal

Vahan Citizen Services — https://vahan.parivahan.gov.in/vahanservice/vahan/

## Steps

1. Go to VAHAN Citizen Services.
2. Enter vehicle registration number.
3. Enter or verify chassis details (last 5 digits).
4. Generate and verify OTP.
5. Select **Duplicate RC**.
6. Enter service details.
7. Update insurance details.
8. Upload required documents.
9. Pay the applicable fee.
10. Book an appointment if required.
11. RTO processes the application.

## Documents commonly required

- Form 26
- Police certificate or report for loss or theft
- Valid insurance
- PUC certificate
- Address proof
- PAN or Form 60/61 where applicable
- Chassis/engine pencil print where required
- Owner signature identification
- Affidavit for lost RC where required

Some documents are state-specific.
