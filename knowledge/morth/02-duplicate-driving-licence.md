# Lost Driving Licence / Duplicate DL

## User questions

- I lost my driving licence
- My DL is lost
- How do I get a duplicate driving licence?
- My driving licence was stolen
- Can I get a duplicate DL online?
- I lost my DL card

## Answer

Apply for a **Duplicate Driving Licence** through the Sarathi Parivahan portal.

## Portal

Sarathi Parivahan — https://parivahan.gov.in

## Steps

1. Visit Sarathi Parivahan.
2. Select your state.
3. Select **Driving Licence**.
4. Choose **Services on Driving Licence**.
5. Enter DL number and date of birth.
6. Select **Duplicate Driving Licence**.
7. Complete the application.
8. Upload documents if requested.
9. Pay the applicable fee.
10. Book an appointment if required.
11. Complete RTO verification if required.

## Documents may include

- Driving licence details or copy, if available
- Identity and address proof
- Application form
- Police report where required by state
- Other state-specific documents
