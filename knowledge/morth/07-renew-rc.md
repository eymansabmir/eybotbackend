# Renew Vehicle Registration (RC)

## User questions

- Renew my RC
- My vehicle registration is expiring
- How do I renew vehicle registration?
- RC expired
- Registration renewal online
- Renew car registration

## Answer

Apply for **Renewal of Registration** through VAHAN for eligible vehicles.

For private non-transport vehicles, apply **not more than 60 days before** the registration expiry date.

## Portal

Vahan — https://vahan.parivahan.gov.in/vahanservice/vahan/

## Steps

1. Open VAHAN Citizen Services.
2. Select **Vehicle Related Services**.
3. Enter vehicle details.
4. Select **Renewal of Registration**.
5. Complete the application.
6. Upload required documents.
7. Pay applicable tax and fees.
8. Complete fitness or inspection requirements where applicable.
9. Complete RTO processing.

## Form

Form 25 — application for renewal of registration certificate.

## Documents may include

- Existing RC
- PUC certificate
- Valid insurance
- Proof of road tax payment
- Fitness certificate where applicable
- PAN or Form 60/61 where applicable
- Chassis/engine pencil print where required

Requirements vary by vehicle type and state.
