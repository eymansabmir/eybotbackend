# Pollution Under Control (PUC) Certificate

## User questions

- Check PUC
- How do I get PUC?
- My PUC expired
- Pollution certificate
- Vehicle pollution certificate
- Where can I get PUC?

## Answer

A valid **Pollution Under Control (PUC)** certificate is required for vehicles as applicable under Indian motor vehicle rules.

## How to obtain

- Visit an authorised PUC testing centre
- Get the vehicle tested
- Obtain and retain the valid PUC certificate
- Renew before expiry

## Notes

PUC is commonly required for RC services such as duplicate RC, renewal, and transfer. Keep PUC valid and upload when VAHAN requests it.
