# Transfer Vehicle Ownership

## User Goal

Transfer vehicle ownership after sale.

## Portal

https://vahan.parivahan.gov.in/vahanservice/vahan/

## Steps

1. Open Vahan Citizen Services.
2. Enter Vehicle Registration Number.
3. Click Proceed.
4. Select "Basic Services".
5. Enter last 5 digits of chassis number.
6. Validate vehicle.
7. Generate OTP.
8. Enter OTP.
9. Select "Transfer Of Ownership".
10. Enter service details.
11. Update insurance details.
12. Complete application submission.

## Required Information

- Vehicle Registration Number
- Chassis Number
- Mobile OTP
- Insurance Details

## Related Questions

- How can I transfer vehicle ownership?
- How do I sell my vehicle?

## Notes

Transfer of ownership application should be initiated by the seller.
Buyer completes subsequent steps.
