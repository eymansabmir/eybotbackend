# Apply For Vehicle NOC

## User questions

- Get NOC for my vehicle
- Vehicle NOC
- NOC for transferring car to another state
- How do I get vehicle NOC?
- Moving my car to another state
- NOC from RTO

## Answer

Apply for a **No Objection Certificate (NOC)** through VAHAN to move a vehicle to another state.

## Portal

Vahan — https://vahan.parivahan.gov.in/vahanservice/vahan/

## Steps

1. Open Vahan Citizen Services.
2. Enter vehicle registration number.
3. Proceed to **Vehicle Related Services**.
4. Select **NOC** service.
5. Complete application information.
6. Submit supporting documents if requested.
7. Pay applicable fee.
8. Track application status online.

## Output

NOC application reference, then NOC approval subject to RTO verification.

## Related

For full interstate move, also see state transfer: NOC from current RTO, then re-registration in destination state.
