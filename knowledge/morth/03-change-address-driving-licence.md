# Change Address on Driving Licence

## User questions

- Change address on my driving licence
- My address has changed
- Update address in DL
- Change address in driving licence online
- I moved to another city, how do I change my DL address?

## Answer

Apply for **Change/Correction of Address in Driving Licence** through Sarathi Parivahan.

## Portal

Sarathi Parivahan — https://parivahan.gov.in

## Steps

1. Open Sarathi Parivahan.
2. Select your state.
3. Select **Driving Licence → Services on Driving Licence**.
4. Enter DL number and date of birth.
5. Select **Change/Correction of Address**.
6. Enter your new address.
7. Upload required address proof.
8. Pay the applicable fee.
9. Book an appointment if required.
10. Complete verification if required.

## Forms

Form 2 includes Change/Correction of Address in Driving Licence.
