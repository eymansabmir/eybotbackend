# Transfer Vehicle To Another State

## User Goal

Relocate vehicle permanently to another state.

## Process

Step 1
Apply for NOC from existing RTO.

Step 2
Move vehicle to destination state.

Step 3
Apply for re-registration through Vahan.

Step 4
Update address details.

Step 5
Pay applicable taxes and fees.

Step 6
Obtain updated registration details.
