# Transfer Vehicle To Another State

## User questions

- Transfer vehicle to another state
- Relocate car
- Interstate registration
- Vehicle re-registration
- Moving car permanently to another state

## Answer

To relocate a vehicle permanently to another state, complete NOC, move, and re-register in the destination state.

## Process overview

1. Apply for **NOC** from the current RTO (VAHAN).
2. Move the vehicle to the destination state.
3. Apply for **re-registration** through VAHAN in the new state.
4. Update **address** details.
5. Pay applicable **taxes and fees**.
6. Obtain updated registration.
