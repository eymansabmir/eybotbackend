# Transfer Vehicle Ownership

## User questions

- Transfer my car to someone else
- Transfer RC to buyer
- How do I transfer vehicle ownership?
- I sold my car
- How to transfer ownership online?
- RC transfer
- Vehicle ownership transfer

## Answer

Vehicle ownership transfer can be initiated through VAHAN Citizen Services.

## Portal

Vahan — https://vahan.parivahan.gov.in/vahanservice/vahan/

## Steps

1. Open VAHAN Citizen Services.
2. Select **Vehicle Related Services**.
3. Enter vehicle registration number and chassis details.
4. Select **Transfer of Ownership**.
5. Enter buyer and seller information.
6. Complete OTP authentication.
7. Upload required documents.
8. Pay applicable fees and taxes.
9. Complete appointment or RTO requirements if applicable.
10. Track the application.

## Forms commonly involved

- Form 29 — notice of transfer of ownership
- Form 30 — report of transfer of ownership

## Notes

Seller should initiate the application; buyer completes subsequent steps. Keep registration number, chassis number, mobile OTP, and insurance details ready.
