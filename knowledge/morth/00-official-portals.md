# Official Government Portals

## Parivahan

URL: https://parivahan.gov.in

Central citizen transport services portal for driving licence and vehicle services.

## Vahan (Citizen Services)

URL: https://vahan.parivahan.gov.in/vahanservice/vahan/

Vehicle registration services: duplicate RC, change of address, transfer of ownership, NOC, registration renewal, hypothecation termination.

## Sarathi

Driving licence services via state selection on the Parivahan portal: renewal, duplicate DL, address change, application status.

## eChallan

URL: https://echallan.parivahan.gov.in/challan/challan-services

Traffic challan search, pending status, and online payment.

## mParivahan

URL: https://mparivahan.parivahan.gov.in

Mobile citizen transport services.

## Services available

- Transfer ownership
- Duplicate RC
- Change of address (RC and DL)
- NOC
- Vehicle tax and registration renewal
- eChallan payment
- Driving licence services
