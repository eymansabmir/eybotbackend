# Official Government Portals

## Parivahan

https://parivahan.gov.in

Purpose:
Central citizen transport services portal.

## Vahan

https://vahan.parivahan.gov.in

Purpose:
Vehicle registration services.

## eChallan

https://echallan.parivahan.gov.in

Purpose:
Traffic challan search and payments.

## mParivahan

https://mparivahan.parivahan.gov.in

Purpose:
Mobile citizen transport services.

- Transfer Ownership
- Duplicate RC
- Change Address
- NOC
- Vehicle Tax
- eChallan
- Driving Licence Services
