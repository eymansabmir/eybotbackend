# Apply For Vehicle NOC

## User Goal

Obtain No Objection Certificate to move vehicle to another state.

## Portal

https://vahan.parivahan.gov.in/vahanservice/vahan/

## Steps

1. Open Vahan Citizen Services.
2. Enter Vehicle Registration Number.
3. Proceed to Vehicle Related Services.
4. Select NOC service.
5. Complete application information.
6. Submit supporting documents if requested.
7. Pay applicable fee.
8. Track application status online.

## Related Questions

- How do I get NOC?
- Moving car to another state.
- Interstate vehicle transfer.
- Vehicle relocation.

## Output

NOC Application Reference.
NOC Approval (subject to RTO verification).
