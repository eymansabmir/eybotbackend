# Check RC / Vehicle Details

## User questions

- Check my RC details
- Check vehicle registration
- Vehicle details by number
- How do I check my RC?
- Check my car registration details
- What is my vehicle registration status?

## Answer

Use VAHAN **Know Your Vehicle Details** to view available registration information.

## Portal

Vahan — https://vahan.parivahan.gov.in

## What you may need

- Vehicle registration number
- Other verification details depending on the service

## Notes

Only provide information required by the official service. Do not share unnecessary sensitive personal data.
