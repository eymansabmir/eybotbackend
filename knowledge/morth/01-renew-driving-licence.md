# Renew Driving Licence

## User questions

- Renew my driving licence
- How do I renew my DL?
- My driving licence is expiring
- My licence expired, can I renew it?
- DL renewal online
- What documents are needed for DL renewal?

## Answer

Apply for driving licence renewal through the **Sarathi Parivahan** portal.

## Portal

Sarathi Parivahan — https://parivahan.gov.in

## Steps

1. Visit the Sarathi Parivahan portal.
2. Select your state.
3. Select **Driving Licence** services.
4. Choose **Services on Driving Licence**.
5. Enter your driving licence number and date of birth.
6. Select **Renewal of Driving Licence**.
7. Fill in the required details.
8. Upload required documents, if requested.
9. Pay the applicable fee.
10. Book an appointment if required.
11. Complete verification at the RTO if required.
12. Track application status online.

## Documents may include

- Existing driving licence
- Form 1 / medical declaration where applicable
- Form 1A medical certificate where applicable
- Address and identity documents where required
- Other documents depending on state, age, and licence category

## Forms

Form 9 — application for renewal of driving licence. Form 2 also includes renewal and other DL services.

## Notes

Requirements vary by applicant age, vehicle category, transport vs non-transport licence, and state RTO rules.
