# Hero Motorcycle RAG Question → Answer Sheet

Use these as evaluation pairs for a basic RAG demo. Answers are intentionally grounded in the Hero-only KB.

## Buyer-intent questions

### Q: I need a bike mainly for office commute and mileage. Which Hero should I consider?
A: Start with the commuter range: HF 100, HF Deluxe, Splendor+, Splendor+ XTEC/XTEC 2.0, Super Splendor XTEC/XTEC 2.0, Glamour, Glamour X and Xtreme 125R. If mileage and low running cost dominate, the Splendor/HF family is the strongest place to start. If you also want sporty styling and stronger performance, consider Xtreme 125R.

### Q: I ride around 20–30 km every day and care mostly about mileage. Which Hero is suitable?
A: The Splendor+ family is a strong fit because Hero lists 70 kmpl with E20 fuel for Splendor+. HF 100 is another budget-focused commuter option and Hero lists 72 kmpl. Actual mileage varies with riding conditions.

### Q: I want the cheapest Hero motorcycle for commuting.
A: HF 100 is the budget-oriented option in this KB. Hero lists the HF 100 at ₹59,839 ex-showroom in Delhi on the retrieved product page. City and variant can change pricing.

### Q: I want a Splendor but with more technology.
A: Look at Splendor+ XTEC or Splendor+ XTEC 2.0. XTEC adds features such as a full digital meter, RTMI, Bluetooth call/SMS alerts, USB charging and i3S. XTEC 2.0 also adds Bluetooth call/SMS alerts, utility storage, RTMI, i3S and LED lighting.

### Q: Splendor vs Xtreme — which should I buy?
A: First clarify which Xtreme. For a mileage-first office commuter, Splendor+ is the more appropriate starting point. Xtreme 125R is better suited if you want a sportier design, stronger 125cc performance, 5-speed transmission, wider rear tyre and features such as ABS on applicable variants. They target different priorities.

### Q: I want mileage but I also want the bike to look sporty.
A: Xtreme 125R is a relevant Hero option. Hero lists 125cc displacement, 11.4 BHP, 10.5 Nm, 5-speed transmission and 63 kmpl WMTC on the current product specification page; another Hero product FAQ cites up to 66 kmpl. Treat the exact mileage figure as source/version dependent and avoid presenting it as a guaranteed real-world result.

### Q: I want a 125cc commuter but not an aggressive bike.
A: Consider Super Splendor XTEC/XTEC 2.0 or Glamour. Super Splendor XTEC is positioned around comfort, mileage and commuting features. Glamour is a 124.7cc stylish commuter with 65 kmpl listed by Hero.

### Q: Which Hero 125cc bike has the highest mileage in this KB?
A: Among the 125cc models represented here, Hero lists Super Splendor XTEC 2.0 at 75 kmpl as a highlighted figure, while Super Splendor XTEC is listed at 69 kmpl, Glamour at 65 kmpl and Xtreme 125R at 63 kmpl WMTC on its specification page. Use the exact source label when answering.

### Q: Which Hero is better for city traffic: Splendor or Xtreme 125R?
A: For pure economy and simple commuting, Splendor+ is the better fit. For a rider who wants a more performance-oriented 125cc bike while still commuting, Xtreme 125R is the better fit. The choice depends on whether fuel economy/simplicity or performance/features has higher priority.

### Q: I need to carry a pillion regularly. What should I consider?
A: Consider comfort-oriented commuter models such as Super Splendor XTEC/XTEC 2.0 or Splendor+. The Super Splendor XTEC source specifically highlights a long, comfortable seat.

### Q: I want a bike for office plus occasional highway rides.
A: Xtreme 125R can be considered if you want a sporty 125cc. For substantially more performance, Xtreme 160R/160R 4V are stronger choices, but they are less focused on minimum running cost than the Splendor family.

### Q: I want a premium-feeling commuter without going too big.
A: Look at Glamour X or Xtreme 125R. Glamour X adds a 10.7 cm multicolor adaptive display, Bluetooth, three ride modes, cruise control and other smart features.

## Feature questions

### Q: Which Splendor has Bluetooth?
A: Splendor+ XTEC and XTEC 2.0 have Bluetooth-related connectivity features in the Hero product material.

### Q: Which Hero commuter has USB charging?
A: Hero highlights USB charging on Splendor+ XTEC and USB Type-C charging on Super Splendor XTEC 2.0. Other products may have different charging provisions.

### Q: Which Hero has ride modes?
A: Glamour X has three ride modes: Eco, Road and Power. Hero also describes three ride modes on the Xtreme 125R product material.

### Q: Which Hero has cruise control?
A: Glamour X is explicitly described by Hero as having cruise control.

### Q: Which Hero has ABS?
A: Xtreme 125R is offered with ABS variants; the current Hero page lists a dual-channel ABS variant and single-channel/other variants. Exact braking equipment depends on variant.

### Q: Which Hero is lightest among the commuter models here?
A: HF 100 is listed at 110 kg kerb weight on the current product page. Always compare the exact variant because equipment can change weight.

## Recommendation guardrails

### Q: Just tell me the best Hero bike.
A: Ask what matters most: budget, daily kilometres, mileage, performance, comfort, pillion use, city traffic, highway use and preferred features. “Best” depends on the rider's priorities.

### Q: I have ₹1 lakh and want maximum mileage.
A: Start by comparing Splendor+, Splendor+ XTEC/XTEC 2.0, Super Splendor XTEC and Xtreme 125R based on the user's priorities and the current city price. Do not claim an exact fit until city and variant are known.

### Q: I don't care about mileage; I want performance.
A: Route toward Xtreme 125R, Xtreme 160R, Xtreme 160R 4V and, for adventure-oriented riding, Xpulse 210.
