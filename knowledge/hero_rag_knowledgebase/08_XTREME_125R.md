# Hero Xtreme 125R

## Product overview
Sporty 125cc motorcycle positioned between basic commuter models and larger performance motorcycles.

## Core specifications
- Engine displacement: 124.7 cc
- Engine: air-cooled 4-stroke
- Max power: 11.4 BHP @ 8250 rpm
- Max torque: 10.5 Nm @ 6500 rpm
- Fuel injection
- Transmission: 5-speed constant mesh
- Acceleration: Hero current product page shows 5.7 sec 0–60 km/h
- WMTC fuel consumption: 63 kmpl on the current specification block
- Fuel tank: 10 L
- Seat height: 790 mm on the current specification block
- Ground clearance: 180 mm
- Wheelbase: 1319 mm
- Kerb mass: 136 kg for self/disc/cast configuration
- Front tyre: 90/90-17 TL
- Rear tyre: 120/80-17 TL
- Rear suspension: adjustable monoshock on current product material
- Braking: variant dependent; Hero lists dual-channel ABS and other braking configurations

## Features / selling points explicitly present in Hero material
