# Hero Splendor+ / Splendor+ XTEC / Splendor+ XTEC 2.0

## Product overview
The Splendor family is Hero's core high-mileage commuter range. The standard Splendor+ emphasizes simplicity and fuel economy; XTEC and XTEC 2.0 add digital/connectivity and convenience features.

## Core specifications
Common core figures across the retrieved Splendor+ family material:
- Engine: 97.2 cc, air-cooled 4-stroke single-cylinder OHC
- Max power: 5.9 kW @ 8000 rpm
- Max torque: 8.05 Nm @ 6000 rpm
- Fuel system: Programmed Fuel Injection
- Transmission: 4-speed constant mesh
- Front tyre: 80/100-18 tubeless
- Rear tyre: 80/100-18 tubeless
- Rear suspension: 5-step adjustable hydraulic shock absorbers
- Ground clearance: 165 mm
- Fuel tank: 9.8 L
- Kerb weight: 112 kg
- Saddle height: 785 mm
- Wheelbase: 1236 mm
