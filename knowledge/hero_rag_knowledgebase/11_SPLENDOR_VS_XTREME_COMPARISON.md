# Splendor vs Xtreme — RAG Comparison

## Important disambiguation

“Xtreme” is ambiguous. Hero has Xtreme 125R, Xtreme 160R and Xtreme 160R 4V in this KB. For an office-commute question mentioning “Splendor vs Xtreme”, retrieve Xtreme 125R first unless the user specifies a different engine/model.

## Recommendation logic

### Choose Splendor+ when:
- Mileage is the top priority.
- The bike is primarily for daily office commuting.
- You prefer simple, practical commuter characteristics.
- You want to minimize purchase cost within this comparison.

### Choose Xtreme 125R when:
- You want noticeably stronger 125cc performance.
- You prefer sporty styling.
- You want a 5-speed gearbox.
- You value features such as ABS availability, wider rear tyre, monoshock and digital display.

### Do not say:
“Xtreme is better than Splendor.”

Instead say:
“Xtreme 125R is better if performance/features matter more; Splendor+ is better if mileage and simple commuting matter more.”
