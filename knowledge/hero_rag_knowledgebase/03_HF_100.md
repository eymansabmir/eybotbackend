# Hero HF 100

## Product overview
Budget-focused commuter motorcycle with Hero's xSENS FI technology, easy-start positioning, safety cut-offs and a simple specification set.

## Core specifications
- Engine: 97.2 cc, air-cooled 4-stroke single-cylinder OHC
- Max power: 5.9 kW @ 8000 rpm
- Max torque: 8.05 Nm @ 6000 rpm
- Fuel system: Advanced Programmed Fuel Injection
- Transmission: 4-speed constant mesh
- Front/rear brakes: 130 mm drum; integrated braking system
- Front suspension: telescopic hydraulic shock absorbers
- Rear suspension: swingarm with 2-step adjustable hydraulic shock absorbers
- Front/rear tyres: 2.75 x 18
- Fuel tank: 9.1 L
- Kerb weight: 110 kg
- Seat height: 805 mm
- Ground clearance: 165 mm
- Wheelbase: 1235 mm
- Starting: kick-start
- Battery: 12V 3Ah
