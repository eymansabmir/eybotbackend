# Hero HF Deluxe

## Product overview
Affordable 97.2cc commuter focused on fuel efficiency and everyday practicality.

## Core specifications
- Engine: 97.2 cc, air-cooled 4-stroke single-cylinder OHC
- Max power: 5.9 kW @ 8000 rpm
- Max torque: 8.05 Nm @ 6000 rpm
- Fuel system: Programmed Fuel Injection
- Transmission: 4-speed constant mesh
- Front/rear brakes: 130 mm drum; integrated braking system
- Front suspension: telescopic hydraulic shock absorbers
- Rear suspension: 2-step adjustable hydraulic shock absorbers
- Front/rear tyres: 80/100-18 tubeless
- Fuel tank: 9.6 L
- Kerb weight: 112 kg
- Seat height: 805 mm
- Ground clearance: 165 mm
- Wheelbase: 1235 mm
- Starting: electric start

## Features / selling points explicitly present in Hero material
- Hero lists 68 kmpl as ARAI-approved mileage
- LED headlamp with HIPL
- i3S on applicable variants
- Multiple commuter-oriented variants
- Practical, fuel-efficient positioning
