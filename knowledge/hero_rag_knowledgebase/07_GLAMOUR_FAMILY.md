# Hero Glamour / Glamour X

## Product overview
125cc commuter family with more styling and feature emphasis than the basic 100cc commuter range.

## Core specifications
### Glamour
- Engine: 124.7 cc, air-cooled 4-stroke
- Max power: 7.75 kW @ 7500 rpm
- Max torque: 10.4 Nm @ 6000 rpm
- Fuel efficiency shown by Hero: 65 km/l
- Fuel system: Fuel Injection
- Front tyre: 80/100-18 tubeless
- Rear tyre: 100/80-18 tubeless
- Alloy wheels
- 5-speed constant mesh

### Glamour X
- Engine: 124.7 cc, air-cooled 4S
- Max power: 8.5 kW @ 8250 rpm
- Max torque: 10.5 Nm @ 6500 rpm
- Hero mileage: up to 65 kmpl with E20
- Fuel tank: 10 L
- Seat height: 790 mm
- Ground clearance: 170 mm
- Wheelbase: 1267 mm
- Kerb weight: 125.5 kg drum / 127 kg disc
- 5-speed/manual transmission

## Features / selling points explicitly present in Hero material
### Glamour X
- 3 ride modes: Eco, Road, Power
- Cruise control
- 10.7 cm multicolor adaptive display
- Bluetooth connectivity
- Panic Brake Alert
- Kickstart in low battery
- Smart storage space
- LED headlamp on the listed configuration
- Long and wide seat
