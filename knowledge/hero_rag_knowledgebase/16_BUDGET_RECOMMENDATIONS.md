# Hero Bikes By Budget (Delhi ex-showroom reference)

## Under ₹80,000

- **HF 100** — budget everyday commuter; Hero Delhi ex-showroom from ₹59,839.
- **HF Deluxe** — fuel-efficient commuter in the budget range.

Best for: lowest purchase price, basic office commute, maximum value.

## ₹80,000 – ₹90,000

- **Splendor+** — high-mileage daily commuter; Hero lists 70 kmpl with E20 fuel.
- **Splendor+ XTEC** — commuter with digital/connectivity features.

Best for: mileage-first riders with some feature interest.

## Around ₹1 lakh (₹90,000 – ₹1,10,000)

- **Splendor+ XTEC 2.0** — upgraded tech commuter.
- **Super Splendor XTEC** — 125cc comfort-oriented commuter; strong mileage figures in Hero material.
- **Glamour** — stylish 125cc commuter.
- **Xtreme 125R** — sporty 125cc if the rider wants performance over pure mileage.

Best for: riders asking "best Hero under 1 lakh" — clarify mileage-first vs sporty preference.

## Answering "which model is best for me under 1 lac"

1. Ask whether mileage, sporty styling, or features matter most (if not stated).
2. Mileage-first under ~₹1L: Splendor+ family, Super Splendor XTEC.
3. Sporty under ~₹1L: Xtreme 125R (check variant/ABS pricing in knowledge).
4. Do not invent on-road price — offer city quote for exact on-road estimate.
5. Prices are ex-showroom Delhi reference unless a city-specific page is retrieved.
