# Hero Super Splendor XTEC / Super Splendor XTEC 2.0

## Product overview
125cc commuter models for riders who want more engine capacity, comfort and technology than the 100cc Splendor family.

## Core specifications
### Super Splendor XTEC
- Engine: 124.7 cc, air-cooled 4-stroke OHC single-cylinder
- Max power: 10.7 BHP @ 7500 rpm
- Max torque: 10.6 Nm @ 6000 rpm
- Fuel system: Advanced Programmed Fuel Injection
- Transmission: 5-speed constant mesh
- Front tyre: 80/100-18 tubeless
- Rear tyre: 90/90-18 tubeless
- Front suspension: telescopic hydraulic shock absorbers
- Rear suspension: 5-step adjustable telescopic hydraulic shock absorbers
- Seat height: 793 mm
- Wheelbase: 1267 mm

### Super Splendor XTEC 2.0
- Engine: 124.7 cc
- Max power: 10.7 kW @ 7500 rpm
- Max torque: 10.6 Nm @ 6000 rpm
