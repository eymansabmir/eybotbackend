# Hero Xtreme 160R / Xtreme 160R 4V

## Product overview
Street-oriented performance motorcycles for riders who want substantially more performance than the 100–125cc commuter range.

## Core specifications
### Xtreme 160R
- Engine: 163.2 cc
- Engine: 4-stroke, air-cooled, 2-valve
- Max power: 15 PS @ 8500 rpm
- Max torque: 14 Nm @ 6500 rpm
- Front tyre: 100/80-17 tubeless
- Rear tyre: 130/70 R17 tubeless
- Rear suspension: 7-step adjustable monoshock

### Xtreme 160R 4V
- Engine: 163.2 cc
- 4-stroke, air-oil cooled, 4-valve
- Max power: 16.9 PS / 12.4 kW @ 8500 rpm
- Max torque: 14.6 Nm @ 6500 rpm
- Bore x stroke: 66.5 x 47.0 mm
- Compression ratio: 10.1:1
- Fuel tank: 12 L

## Features / selling points explicitly present in Hero material
- Xtreme 160R: street performance positioning, alloy wheels, monoshock
- Xtreme 160R 4V: 4-valve engine, performance-focused chassis/braking and wide radial rear tyre
- 4V variants include standard Xtreme 160R 4V and ETB in the retrieved Hero material
