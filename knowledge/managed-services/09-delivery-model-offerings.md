# Global Delivery Model and MS Offerings

Source grounding: MS GTM (July 2026). Tagline: EY Managed Services — It's your world, running on ours, better.

## Global Managed Services delivery model

EY's global Managed Services delivery model provides 24/7 expertise through a network of centers with optimized staffing (local, nearshore, offshore) and language support.

Regional notes:

- Latin America: Mexico and Argentina — Spanish/Portuguese expertise and 24-hour support.
- Europe: Poland and Spain — language-based support.
- India: largest centre of excellence with deep Tax and Technology expertise.

Service hubs referenced include Hungary, Poland, Spain, UAE, India, South Africa, Philippines, China, Mexico, Costa Rica and Argentina.

## Client value themes

- Lower cost: standardized services plus global delivery reduce run cost and improve resilience.
- Talent at scale: specialist domain, technology and transformation skills in flexible run teams.
- Modern operations: stabilize apps, data and controls; automate tickets, RCA and testing.
- Outcome governance: KPI/SLA baselines, scorecards, QBR decisions and benefits tracking.

## Phased MS approach

1. Diagnose: scope and baseline.
2. Stabilize: BAU.
3. Transform: AI and continuous improvement.

Lifecycle path: Baseline Assessment → Shadow → Reverse Shadow → BAU → Innovation → Benefits Realization.

## Three-pronged approach

**Design and Transition:** scope, operating model, migration, delivery setup, GRC framework, KPI/SLA baselines focused on value.

**Operate and Stabilize:** end-to-end delivery, KPI/SLA performance, governance cadence, quality/risk/compliance.

**Optimize and Transform:** continuous improvement, automation/AI layering, cost and productivity uplift, analytics, transformation office.

## North Star operating model

North Star: a connected, intelligent and future-ready enterprise enabled by AI-led Managed Services.

Four pillars:

1. Stabilize the Core — reliable, secure performance and transition continuity.
2. Build Future Ready Tech — scalable platforms and modern architecture.
3. Enable Intelligent Ops — AI-led operations and real-time visibility.
4. Deliver Business Value — measurable outcomes, innovation and cost efficiency.

## CXO IT challenges and EY answers

Challenges: IT spend not rationalized; critical talent gaps; fragmented IT operations; AI not scaling beyond pilots; business alignment and flexibility.

EY answers: AI dividend platforms; access to global talent bench; deep functional knowledge; link IT to business outcomes via KPIs/SLAs; improve scale through India delivery plus onshore/offshore.
