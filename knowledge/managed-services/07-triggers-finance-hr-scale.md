# Customer Triggers — Finance, HR, Coverage and Scale

## “Finance spends too much time on reconciliations and close.”

- **What it may indicate:** Transactional effort and manual close activities.
- **EY MS conversation move:** Position managed finance operations covering processing, reconciliation, reporting, controls and optimization.
- **Value to position:** Faster close, reduced effort and improved control.
- **Discovery question:** Which close activities are most labour-intensive, late or error-prone?

## “HR receives the same employee queries repeatedly.”

- **What it may indicate:** High-volume repeatable service demand.
- **EY MS conversation move:** Offer employee service operations using tiered support, knowledge, self-service, workflow and AI-enabled handling.
- **Value to position:** Faster and more consistent employee support.
- **Discovery question:** What proportion of requests are repetitive versus requiring specialist judgement?

## “We need 24×7 support, but an internal model is too expensive.”

- **What it may indicate:** Coverage requirement exceeds current economics.
- **EY MS conversation move:** Explore a shared/global model with follow-the-sun coverage, severity SLAs, on-call arrangements and escalation.
- **Value to position:** Broader coverage and predictable service.
- **Discovery question:** Which services genuinely require 24×7 coverage and what recovery targets are critical?

## “We are entering new markets and cannot build operations fast enough.”

- **What it may indicate:** Need for rapid, repeatable scaling.
- **EY MS conversation move:** Position modular operations with standard processes, scalable capacity and local compliance support where appropriate.
- **Value to position:** Faster mobilisation and lower set-up burden.
- **Discovery question:** Which capabilities must be live first, and what country and volume requirements apply?

## “We are considering a GCC but are unsure how to build and operate it.”

- **What it may indicate:** Operating-model, talent and mobilisation challenge.
- **EY MS conversation move:** Discuss build-operate-transfer, managed GCC or capability-centre constructs across transition, talent, process, governance and technology.
- **Value to position:** Faster capability creation and controlled transition.
- **Discovery question:** Is the priority speed, long-term ownership, cost optimization or a combination?
