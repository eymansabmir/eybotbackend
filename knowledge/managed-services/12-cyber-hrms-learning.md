# Cyber, HRMS, Payroll and Managed Learning

Source grounding: MS GTM — Cyber MS, HRMS catalogue, Managed Learning Services.

## Cyber Managed Services

Long-term (often 1–5 year) constructs where EY implements, operates and continuously manages cybersecurity and IT services with end-to-end accountability for technology, processes and outcomes.

Services offered include:

- Threat Detection and Response (TDR).
- Cyber Automation and Threat Hunting.
- Security Platform operations.
- Identity and Access Management.
- CPM, Inventory Insights, AI-R&SPM.
- Testing and Assurance.
- Data Discovery and Classification.
- Incident Investigation.

Typical talk tracks: 24x7 managed SOC; operate and optimize multiple cybersecurity platforms; monitoring, governance, platform engineering and automated cyber resilience.

## HRMS / Employee Help-Desk

Employee Help-Desk is a centralized channel for employment-related information and resources from onboarding to retirement, via call, email and chat, with case intake, reassignment/escalation, inquiry management, workforce management and SLA/KPI governance.

Broader HRMS catalogue themes:

- People strategy, global mobility, learning, policies and process.
- Compensation, employee relations and compliance, talent management.
- Change management, employee data management, payroll, workforce management.
- Recruitment, separation, application management.

## Managed Learning Services (MLS)

MLS combines strategy, skills baselining and operated learning services (including Spotmentor for skills gaps and personalized paths), learning technology, logistics, help desk, admin services, content development and trainer delivery.

Phases:

1. Learning Strategy — advisory, program management, change.
2. Skills Transformation and Baselining — assessments and career pathways.
3. Managed Learning Services 2.0 — deploy, stabilize, maintain.

GTM claim: EY Managed Learning Services supports operations in 150+ countries and 30 languages.

## When to route to these domains

- SOC / TDR / cyber platforms / identity → Cyber Managed Services.
- HR helpdesk, payroll, employee service ops → HRMS / Payroll.
- Learning ops, skills transformation, managed learning platforms → Managed Learning.
