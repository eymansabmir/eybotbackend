# Managed Services Definition and Benefits

Source grounding: MS Brief (June 2026). Tagline: EY Managed Services — It's your world, running on ours, better.

## Why Managed Services

Clients need a target operating model that is strategic, cost-effective, technology-enabled and supported by best-in-class resources. Market pressures include:

- Aggressive near-term cost reduction and greater agility.
- Difficulty finding skilled resources at reasonable cost.
- Limited investment capacity for sustainable data and technology transformation.
- Continual ask to take on more without adequate resourcing.
- Need to automate and keep pace with peers without technology capex.

## EY definition of Managed Services

EY Managed Services provides expertise-led services to run business-critical processes and functions for clients, leveraging specialists, technology, automation and innovation to drive significant value.

Engagement characteristics typically include:

- Multi-year and outcome driven.
- Defined KPIs and measurable service levels.
- Fees at risk where commercially appropriate.
- Recurring revenue.
- Pricing may include unit-based, input-based, output-based, outcome-based, fixed-fee or subscription models.
- Delivered in a leveraged shared-services model, often with offshore resources.
- Uses data insights for continuous improvement.

## Benefits for clients

- Access critical talent and technology.
- Shared risk, including diversified delivery and potential outcomes-based commercials for certain offerings.
- Innovate at pace through scaling and tech-enabled services.
- Reduce cost to operate through operational efficiencies and lower total cost of ownership.

## Benefits for EY

- Address client needs and access new revenue streams.
- Expand traditional business into sticky, multi-year partnerships.
- Differentiate EY with outcome-led operating relationships.

A Channel 2 priority account delivering a managed service has, on average, 4.3x higher GTER and 1.2x less volatility than an account without a managed service (per MS Brief).

## High-potential account characteristics

Ideal signals:

- Trusted C-suite relationships and culture that can embrace an MS operating model.
- Alignment with EY brand permission and related current work.
- Strong appetite from at least one Executive to challenge the existing operating model.
- Clear and important operating challenge.

Positive triggers include new Executive change, talent/quality gaps, cost pressure, incumbent fatigue, risk reduction, desire to free internal capacity for strategy, avoid tech investment, or carve-out / strategic transaction.

Red flags:

- Strong do-it-yourself mentality.
- Labor restrictions that block the model.
- Surprise RFP with pure BPO focus.
- Entrenched incumbent position.
- Significant existing offshore use with little room to differentiate.
