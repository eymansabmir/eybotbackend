# Customer Triggers — Technology, Cloud, Security and Data

## “Our application team only has time to keep the lights on.”

- **What it may indicate:** Technical debt and innovation backlog.
- **EY MS conversation move:** Propose run-and-transform AMS covering operations, engineering, backlog reduction, automation and modernization.
- **Value to position:** Stability plus capacity for innovation.
- **Discovery question:** How much capacity is spent on incidents versus enhancements and modernization?

## “The new system is live, but we are struggling to stabilise it.”

- **What it may indicate:** Need for post-transformation operations.
- **EY MS conversation move:** Extend the programme into stabilization, application support, adoption, optimization and backlog management.
- **Value to position:** Protect transformation investment and accelerate benefits.
- **Discovery question:** Who owns the platform after hypercare, and how will optimization be governed?

## “We bought tools, but adoption and utilisation remain low.”

- **What it may indicate:** Technology investment is not producing value.
- **EY MS conversation move:** Offer platform operations covering adoption, administration, optimization, release management and value tracking.
- **Value to position:** Improved return on investment and feature adoption.
- **Discovery question:** Which capabilities are underused, and how are realized benefits measured?

## “Automation and AI pilots are not scaling into BAU.”

- **What it may indicate:** Innovation lacks operational ownership.
- **EY MS conversation move:** Offer AI-enabled operations with use-case intake, controlled deployment, human oversight, measurement and refinement.
- **Value to position:** Sustainable productivity and institutionalized innovation.
- **Discovery question:** Who owns each use case after pilot, and how are accuracy, controls and benefits monitored?

## “Our processes depend on spreadsheets and emails.”

- **What it may indicate:** Manual, fragmented and weakly controlled workflows.
- **EY MS conversation move:** Propose process discovery followed by workflow-enabled operations, automation and exception management.
- **Value to position:** Traceability, shorter turnaround and fewer errors.
- **Discovery question:** Which activities are rules-based, repetitive and most prone to rework?

## “Security alerts are increasing and we cannot investigate everything.”

- **What it may indicate:** High-volume monitoring and capacity constraint.
- **EY MS conversation move:** Discuss managed monitoring, triage, escalation, threat intelligence and incident workflow.
- **Value to position:** Improved coverage and consistent response.
- **Discovery question:** What is the alert volume, investigation backlog and critical-system profile?

## “Cloud cost is increasing, but we cannot explain why.”

- **What it may indicate:** Weak consumption governance and optimization.
- **EY MS conversation move:** Position continuous FinOps operations covering attribution, tagging, optimization, forecasting and governance.
- **Value to position:** Cost transparency and continuous optimization.
- **Discovery question:** Can consumption be attributed to business owners, products and environments?

## “Applications or infrastructure experience frequent downtime.”

- **What it may indicate:** Reactive maintenance or weak observability.
- **EY MS conversation move:** Offer observability, incident/problem management, capacity management and reliability improvement against business-service KPIs.
- **Value to position:** Improved resilience and faster recovery.
- **Discovery question:** Which services create the greatest business impact when unavailable?

## “Our data is inconsistent and takes too long to reconcile.”

- **What it may indicate:** Recurring data-quality and operational problem.
- **EY MS conversation move:** Shape managed data operations covering ingestion monitoring, quality rules, reconciliation, exceptions and stewardship support.
- **Value to position:** Trusted data and less manual reconciliation.
- **Discovery question:** Where do data breaks occur, and which decisions or reports are affected?
