# Purpose and MS Qualification Lens

Purpose of this document is to help EY teams recognize customer statements that may indicate a Managed Services opportunity and convert them into a structured, value-led conversation.

## Three tests before positioning Managed Services

Use these three tests before positioning Managed Services:

- **Run / Operate Scope:** The activity is recurring and can be operated, not merely a one-off advisory or implementation task.
- **Measurable Service Delivery:** Outputs and performance can be governed through KPIs, SLAs, controls and clear accountability.
- **Transition Feasibility:** Scope, knowledge, access, governance and operating responsibilities can be transitioned responsibly.

Only when these tests are satisfied should EY shape a Managed Services construct.
