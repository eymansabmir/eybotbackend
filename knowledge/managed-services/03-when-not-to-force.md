# When Not to Force an MS Construct

Do **not** force a Managed Services construct when:

- The requirement is purely one-off advisory or implementation work.
- The client only wants named resources under its direct day-to-day control.
- There is no recurring service, operational ownership or measurable output.
- There is no sponsor, transition appetite, governance or funding.
- The requested construct would transfer responsibilities that must remain with client management.
- The process baseline, dependencies or data are insufficient to price or accept outcome risk.

In these cases, stay in advisory, implementation, or staff-augmentation framing rather than positioning Managed Services.
