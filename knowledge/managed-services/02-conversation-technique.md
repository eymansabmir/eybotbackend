# Recommended EY Conversation Technique

Use this six-step technique in client conversations about Managed Services:

1. **Acknowledge the pain** — Reflect the client’s operational challenge in business language.
2. **Quantify the impact** — Establish volumes, backlog, cost, errors, downtime, complaints, risk exposure and internal effort. Avoid unsupported savings claims.
3. **Test MS suitability** — Confirm the scope is recurring, measurable, transitionable and governable.
4. **Reframe resources to results** — Move from “How many FTEs?” to “What outputs, service levels, controls and improvements are required?”
5. **Shape the value construct** — Combine domain expertise, scalable delivery, technology, automation, governance and continuous improvement.
6. **Close a practical next step** — Agree a diagnostic, discovery workshop, baseline exercise or pilot for one process, service tower or geography.
