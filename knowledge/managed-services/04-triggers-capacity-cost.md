# Customer Triggers — Capacity, Cost and Skills

## “Our team spends most of its time on BAU and firefighting.”

- **What it may indicate:** Recurring operations are consuming strategic capacity.
- **EY MS conversation move:** Offer an EY-operated BAU service with defined scope, SLAs, governance, automation and continuous improvement.
- **Value to position:** Release retained capacity; improve discipline and predictability.
- **Discovery question:** Which recurring activities consume the most capacity, and what strategic work is being delayed?

## “We need to reduce operating cost this year.”

- **What it may indicate:** Immediate efficiency or productivity mandate.
- **EY MS conversation move:** Baseline demand, effort, process variation, automation potential, location mix and unit cost; shape a productivity-led MS construct.
- **Value to position:** Predictable cost, productivity improvement and benefits transparency.
- **Discovery question:** Is the priority cost alone, or must service quality, controls and experience also improve?

## “We cannot hire or retain the required skills.”

- **What it may indicate:** Specialist capability gap or unstable capacity.
- **EY MS conversation move:** Provide access to a scalable pool of domain, technology and operational specialists rather than client-by-client hiring.
- **Value to position:** Access to scarce skills, continuity and lower key-person dependency.
- **Discovery question:** Which skills create delivery bottlenecks, and are the needs recurring or seasonal?

## “A few critical people hold all the knowledge.”

- **What it may indicate:** Concentration and business-continuity risk.
- **EY MS conversation move:** Position knowledge transition, SOPs, knowledge management, cross-skilling and succession coverage.
- **Value to position:** Institutionalized knowledge and stronger resilience.
- **Discovery question:** What would happen if those individuals were unavailable during a critical period?

## “Volumes fluctuate during month-end, year-end or peak seasons.”

- **What it may indicate:** Demand variability makes fixed capacity inefficient.
- **EY MS conversation move:** Explore scalable capacity or transaction pricing with volume bands and surge mechanisms.
- **Value to position:** Flexible capacity and reduced idle-cost exposure.
- **Discovery question:** What are the normal, peak and exceptional volumes, and how much advance visibility is available?

## “Turnaround time is inconsistent and the backlog keeps increasing.”

- **What it may indicate:** Capacity, workflow or governance issue.
- **EY MS conversation move:** Offer an output-led service governed by backlog, ageing and turnaround SLAs, supported by standardization and automation.
- **Value to position:** Faster processing and clearer accountability.
- **Discovery question:** What are the current backlog, ageing profile, required TAT and main delay drivers?

## “The business expects more without additional resources.”

- **What it may indicate:** Demand-capacity imbalance.
- **EY MS conversation move:** Offer demand management, service catalogue, automation and tiered delivery through a scalable MS construct.
- **Value to position:** Demand control, scalable capacity and transparent prioritization.
- **Discovery question:** Which requests are recurring, and how are they prioritized against capacity?

## “We need predictable costs rather than continuous change requests.”

- **What it may indicate:** Need for commercial certainty and clear boundaries.
- **EY MS conversation move:** Explore fixed-fee, subscription, capacity-band or unit pricing with baselines, volume assumptions and change mechanisms.
- **Value to position:** Budget predictability and commercial transparency.
- **Discovery question:** How stable are scope and volumes, and what demand drivers could change cost?

## “We need a partner to continuously improve—not just provide resources.”

- **What it may indicate:** Direct opening for mature Managed Services.
- **EY MS conversation move:** Shape an outcome-led service with productivity commitments, automation pipeline and innovation governance.
- **Value to position:** Continuous value improvement rather than staff augmentation.
- **Discovery question:** Which improvement outcomes should the provider own, and how will they be measured?
