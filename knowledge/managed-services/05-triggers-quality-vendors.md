# Customer Triggers — Quality, Experience, Vendors and Governance

## “Service quality varies by location, team or vendor.”

- **What it may indicate:** Fragmented processes and inconsistent controls.
- **EY MS conversation move:** Standardise SOPs, QA controls, training, service levels and central governance.
- **Value to position:** Consistent quality, fewer errors and improved visibility.
- **Discovery question:** Where is variation highest, and is quality measured consistently?

## “We receive too many complaints or escalations.”

- **What it may indicate:** Weak service experience or issue resolution.
- **EY MS conversation move:** Shape an experience-led MS model with omnichannel intake, case management, knowledge-enabled resolution and experience KPIs.
- **Value to position:** Faster resolution and fewer repeat contacts.
- **Discovery question:** What are the top complaint categories, repeat-contact rates and escalation drivers?

## “We meet the SLA, but the business is still unhappy.”

- **What it may indicate:** Metrics measure activity rather than business value.
- **EY MS conversation move:** Redesign scorecards around experience, effectiveness, availability and first-time-right—not response time alone.
- **Value to position:** Align service performance with business expectations.
- **Discovery question:** Which business outcomes or user experiences are missing from the current SLA framework?

## “We have multiple vendors, but no one owns the end-to-end outcome.”

- **What it may indicate:** Fragmented accountability and hand-offs.
- **EY MS conversation move:** Discuss service integration, end-to-end process ownership, integrated reporting and cross-vendor governance.
- **Value to position:** Clear ownership and faster resolution of cross-vendor issues.
- **Discovery question:** Where do issues move between providers without a clear owner?

## “We are paying several vendors and do not know whether we get value.”

- **What it may indicate:** Poor spend visibility and supplier fragmentation.
- **EY MS conversation move:** Offer service baselining, supplier rationalization, should-cost analysis and performance governance.
- **Value to position:** Cost transparency, reduced overlap and stronger accountability.
- **Discovery question:** Can spend, scope, performance and contractual commitments be linked by supplier?

## “Our reporting is manual and leadership lacks a reliable view.”

- **What it may indicate:** Fragmented data and limited operational visibility.
- **EY MS conversation move:** Position an MS control tower with automated dashboards, demand/SLA reporting, root-cause analysis and benefits tracking.
- **Value to position:** Trusted management information and proactive decisions.
- **Discovery question:** Which decisions are delayed because data is unavailable or manually consolidated?

## “We repeatedly face the same incidents or errors.”

- **What it may indicate:** Reactive operations without problem management.
- **EY MS conversation move:** Shift from ticket closure to RCA, permanent fixes, knowledge reuse and automation.
- **Value to position:** Lower repeat demand and improved stability.
- **Discovery question:** What proportion of demand is repeatable, and how are permanent fixes prioritized?

## “Audit observations keep recurring.”

- **What it may indicate:** Controls are not embedded in BAU.
- **EY MS conversation move:** Position a control-enabled MS model with evidence management, exception monitoring and remediation tracking.
- **Value to position:** Audit readiness and reduced repeat findings.
- **Discovery question:** Which findings recur, who owns remediation, and how is evidence managed?

## “Regulatory requirements change too quickly for our team.”

- **What it may indicate:** Need for sustained specialist capability.
- **EY MS conversation move:** Explore domain-led operations combining monitoring, impact assessment, execution, controls and reporting, within applicable responsibility boundaries.
- **Value to position:** Specialist access, consistent execution and compliance visibility.
- **Discovery question:** Which activities are recurring, and which decisions must remain with management?
