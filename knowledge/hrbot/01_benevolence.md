# Benevolence Policy

## Purpose

The Benevolence Policy provides financial assistance to employees facing significant financial hardship due to medical emergencies, accidents, or other exceptional circumstances.

## Eligibility

### Eligible Applicants

The following individuals may apply:

- Confirmed on-roll employees who have completed probation (if applicable)
- Fixed-Term Employees (FTEs) with a minimum of 3 months of service
- Articled Interns

### Not Eligible

- Employees serving notice period

---

## Qualifying Circumstances

Financial assistance may be considered for hardship arising from:

- Severe illness
- Expensive medical treatment
- Critical injury
- Accident-related loss

The circumstances may impact either:

- The employee, or
- Immediate family members (direct ascendants or descendants)

### Additional Considerations

The listed circumstances are not exhaustive. The Benevolence Committee may evaluate any hardship request that aligns with the intent of the policy.

---

## Financial Assistance

### Coverage Limits

The Benevolence Committee may approve assistance subject to the following limits:

- Up to two-thirds (66.67%) of the actual expense incurred.
- Expenses already covered by:
  - Employee medical insurance
  - Organization medical insurance
  - Immediate family member medical insurance
  
  are not eligible for reimbursement.

### Maximum Assistance

The total benefit approved cannot exceed:

- Three times the employee's last drawn annual fixed compensation

The approved amount may be disbursed over:

- Up to 3 years

### Minimum Request Value

Normally, the requested amount should be at least:

- 25% of the employee's current annual fixed compensation

Exceptions may be approved based on the employee's financial circumstances.

### Payment Method

- Bank transfer only
- Applicable taxes may apply

---

## Exceptional Hardship Cases

For hardship situations not explicitly covered under this policy, the Benevolence Committee may approve:

- Grant
- Loan
- Combination of grant and loan

Approval is based on individual case assessment.

---

## Benevolence Committee

### Composition

- Internal committee consisting of 6 senior leaders

### Approval Requirement

- Minimum quorum of 4 members required for approval decisions

### Authority

The Benevolence Committee:

- Reviews all benevolence requests
- Determines eligibility
- Approves or rejects requests
- Decides assistance type and amount
- Acts as the final authority for policy interpretation and disputes

---

## Application Process

### Request Submission

A request may be initiated by:

- The eligible employee, or
- Another employee acting on their behalf

### Submission Requirements

Requests must be submitted to:

- Reporting Partner
- HR / Talent Team

Requests may be submitted through:

- Email
- Physical written application

### Supporting Documentation

Applicants should provide evidence supporting the request, such as:

- Medical reports
- Hospital records
- Bills and invoices
- Expense estimates
- Other relevant supporting documents

### Review Timeline

1. Reporting Partner and HR review the request.
2. Approved requests are submitted to the Benevolence Committee.
3. Committee reviews the request within 7 working days.
4. Committee determines the assistance amount and type within 7 working days of approval.

### Emergency Cases

Where emergency funds are required:

- Application may be submitted after fund disbursement if necessary.

---

## Applicant Declaration Requirements

Applicants must:

- Confirm that provided information is accurate and complete.
- Consent to verification of submitted information.
- Allow necessary information to be shared with the Benevolence Committee.

Providing false information may result in:

- Denial of assistance
- Disciplinary action

---

## Important Notes

- Benevolence assistance is a discretionary employee welfare benefit.
- Approval is not automatic and is subject to Committee review.
- All decisions of the Benevolence Committee are final.
- The organization may modify or discontinue the policy at its discretion.
