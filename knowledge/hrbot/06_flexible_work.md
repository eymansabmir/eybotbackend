# Flexible Work Arrangement Policy

## Purpose

The Flexible Work Arrangement Policy provides employees with alternative work arrangements that support personal and family needs while ensuring business continuity and productivity.

The policy enables flexibility regarding:

- Working hours
- Work location
- Work schedule

---

# Eligibility

Applicable to:

- Employees on the rolls of the organization in India

Not applicable to:

- Interns
- Trainees
- Contract employees

---

# Available Flexible Work Options

The policy offers four flexible working arrangements:

1. Flexi-time Arrangement
2. Part-time Working
3. Telecommuting for Working Mothers
4. Work From Home

---

# 1. Flexi-Time Arrangement

## Purpose

Allows employees flexibility in choosing start and end work timings while maintaining:

- 40 working hours per week
- 8.5 working hours per day (including lunch break)

---

## Standard Office Hours

### Delhi NCR

- 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM

### All Other Locations

- 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM

---

## Available Flexi-Time Slots

Employees may choose one of the following approved schedules:

- 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM
- 10:30 AM to 7:00 PM

---

## Approval Process

Employees must:

1. Discuss the requirement with their Reporting Manager or Counselor.
2. Obtain manager approval before starting the arrangement.
3. Submit the approved email to Location HR / Service Line HR for records.

A new approval is required whenever the employee wishes to change the selected time slot.

---

## Conditions

- Available on a temporary basis.
- Duration must be agreed with the Reporting Manager.
- Client and business requirements must continue to be met.
- Salary, benefits, and other employment terms remain unchanged.
- Travel policy and other organization policies continue to apply.

---

# 2. Part-Time Working

## Purpose

Allows employees to reduce working hours to:

- 4 working hours per day
- Excluding lunch break

---

## Eligibility

Employee must have completed:

- At least 52 weeks of service

---

## Approval Process

Employees must:

1. Discuss the request with their Reporting Manager or Counselor.
2. Raise the request at least 1 month before the proposed start date.
3. Obtain Reporting Partner approval.
4. Submit approval email to Location HR.

Employees wishing to discontinue the arrangement must provide:

- 1 month's notice

---

## Duration

Minimum commitment:

- 6 months

Extensions:

- Require re-application
- Granted in additional 6-month blocks

---

## Conditions

### Working Hours

Employees cannot compress their workweek.

Not permitted:

- Working 20 hours across 3 or 4 days

Required:

- 4 working hours per day

---

### Compensation & Benefits

During the arrangement:

- Compensation is proportionately reduced.
- Benefits may be adjusted proportionately.
- CTC is restructured based on reduced working hours.

---

### Leave Rules

#### Leave Usage

When taking leave:

- 4 hours per day should be charged in timesheets.

#### Leave Accrual

Leave accrues proportionately.

Example:

- 11 half-day leaves over a 6-month period
- Equivalent to 5.5 full-day leaves

---

### Business Requirement Exception

Employees may be required to work beyond 4 hours per day for:

- Critical client needs
- Business requirements

Such expectations should be discussed and agreed with the Reporting Partner.

---

# 3. Telecommuting for Working Mothers

## Purpose

Allows eligible working mothers to split work between office and home.

Structure:

- 4 hours in office
- 4 hours from home

Excludes:

- Lunch break
- Travel time between office and home

---

## Eligibility

Eligible employees:

- Working mothers employed in India
- Employees with at least 52 weeks of service

Not eligible:

- Interns
- Trainees
- Contract employees

---

## Approval Process

Employees must:

1. Discuss the request with their Reporting Manager or Counselor.
2. Raise the request at least 1 month before the proposed start date.
3. Obtain Reporting Partner approval.
4. Submit approval email to Location HR.

Employees wishing to discontinue must provide:

- 1 month's notice

---

## Duration

Initial duration:

- 6 months

Extension:

- May be reapplied for
- Additional 6-month periods

---

## Conditions

Employees must:

- Maintain a suitable work environment at home.
- Remain connected through email during work hours.
- Remain reachable by phone during work hours.

Managers must ensure required equipment is provided, including:

- Laptop
- Data card
- RSA access
- Other required tools

---

## Business Requirement Exception

Employees may be required to work beyond the agreed schedule where necessary for:

- Critical client requirements
- Business obligations

---

## Compensation Impact

- No change to employment status.
- Other terms and conditions remain unchanged.

---

# 4. Work From Home (WFH)

## Purpose

Allows employees to perform work responsibilities from a location other than the office while maintaining regular working hours.

---

## Eligibility

Applicable to:

- All employees
- Interns

---

## Approval Process

Employees must:

1. Discuss the requirement with their Reporting Manager.
2. Obtain approval for each Work From Home request.

Approval is based on:

- Client commitments
- Business requirements
- Team needs

---

## Duration

Employees may request:

- 1 to 5 days at a time

Each request is reviewed separately.

A new approval is required for every Work From Home instance.
