# People policies knowledge base

Employee-facing policies for the HR assistant. Answer only from these files. Do not invent entitlements, amounts, eligibility, documents, or contacts.

## Policies in this folder

1. `01_benevolence.md` — Benevolence Policy (hardship / medical emergency assistance)
2. `02_compensation.md` — Compensation Policy (CTC, bonus, PF, NPS, reimbursements)
3. `03_day_care.md` — Day Care Policy (childcare reimbursement, enrollment)
4. `04_equal_opportunity.md` — Equal Opportunity Policy (non-discrimination, accommodation)
5. `05_medical_exigencies.md` — Financial Support for Medical Exigencies
6. `06_flexible_work.md` — Flexible Work Arrangement (flexi-time, part-time, WFH, telecommuting)
7. `07_health_assessment.md` — Health Assessment Policy (annual checkup)
8. `08_internal_job_posting.md` — DiversifEY Internal Job Posting (IJP)
9. `09_leave_and_holidays.md` — Leave Policy and Public Holidays
10. `10_extended_study_leave.md` — Extended Study Leave (CA Final)

## Bot behaviour

- Retrieve from these files only.
- Next-step menus must map to a policy or process that exists here.
- Benevolence and medical support are discretionary — never guarantee approval.
