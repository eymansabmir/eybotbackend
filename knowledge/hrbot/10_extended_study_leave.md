# Extended Study Leave Policy

## Purpose

The Extended Study Leave Policy supports employees who have completed their articleship but have not yet cleared the CA Final examination.

The policy provides dedicated unpaid leave to help eligible employees focus on preparation for the CA Final examination, recognizing it as an important career milestone.

---

# Eligibility

The policy applies to employees who:

- Have completed 3 years of articleship.
- Have not yet cleared the CA Final examination.
- Are currently employed in one of the following roles:
  - Client Support Specialist (CSS) in Assurance and Advisory
  - Intern 4 in Tax

---

# Leave Benefit

Eligible employees may avail:

- Unpaid leave
- Maximum duration: 6 months

The leave is intended exclusively for:

- Preparation for CA Final examinations

---

# Conditions

## Availability Period

The leave may be availed:

- Within 1 year of completing articleship

---

## Study Leave Restriction

Once an employee opts for Extended Study Leave:

- They become ineligible for any other study-related leave during that period.

---

## Leave Calculation

The leave duration is calculated using:

- Continuous calendar days

This means the following are included when calculating leave duration and Loss of Pay (LOP):

- Weekdays
- Weekends
- Public holidays

Example:

If an employee takes leave from 1 January to 30 June, all calendar days during that period count toward the leave entitlement.

---

# Approval Process

Employees must obtain approval from:

- Counselor
- Engagement Manager

before proceeding on leave.

### Timeline

Approval should be sought:

- At least 15 days before the intended leave start date.

---

# Submission Process

After receiving approvals:

1. Submit the leave request through the Leave Management System.
2. Inform the relevant:
   - Location HR
   - Service Line HR

before commencing the leave.

---

# Key Rules Summary

- Applicable to CSS (Assurance & Advisory) and Intern 4 (Tax) employees.
- Employee must have completed 3 years of articleship.
- Employee must not have cleared CA Final examination.
- Maximum leave available: 6 months.
- Leave is unpaid.
- Leave can only be availed within 1 year of completing articleship.
- No other study-related leave can be availed once Extended Study Leave is chosen.
- Leave is calculated using continuous calendar days, including weekends and public holidays.
- Counselor and Engagement Manager approval is mandatory.
- Approval should be obtained at least 15 days before leave commencement.
