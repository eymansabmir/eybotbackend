# Health Assessment Policy

## Purpose

The Health Assessment Policy provides employees with a complimentary annual health checkup as part of the organization's wellbeing initiative.

The objective is to promote preventive healthcare and encourage employees to monitor their overall health and wellness.

---

# Eligibility

## Eligible Employees

The complimentary annual health assessment is available to:

- On-roll employees

---

## Not Eligible

The following categories are excluded:

- Partners
- Interns
- Contractual employees
- Off-roll personnel
- Retainers

---

## Family Member Benefit

Employees may also avail health assessments for:

- Immediate family members

Coverage:

- Up to 4 family members

Family member assessments are available at:

- Subsidized rates

---

# Health Checkup Coverage

The annual health assessment includes:

- 11 health profiles
- Approximately 67 diagnostic tests

---

## Included Health Profiles

The health assessment covers:

1. Lipid Profile
2. Iron Studies
3. Vitamin B12
4. Vitamin D
5. HbA1c
6. Blood Glucose (Fasting)
7. Thyroid Profile
8. Kidney Function Test
9. Liver Function Test
10. Urine Routine & Microscopy
11. Complete Hemogram

---

# Service Provider

Health assessments are delivered through:

- Tata 1MG

Employees can access the service using:

- Tata 1MG App
- Tata 1MG Website

---

# Availability

Employees can avail the complimentary assessment:

- Once per eligible assessment cycle

Booking is subject to:

- Serviceability of the employee's location/pincode

---

# Sample Collection Options

Employees may choose:

- Home sample collection
- Sample collection at EY office location

---

# Appointment Scheduling

Employees may:

- Choose preferred date and time
- Select weekday or weekend appointments

subject to slot availability.

---

# Corporate Booking Benefits

Only tests available through the organization's corporate health dashboard are eligible for:

- Complimentary employee testing
- Subsidized dependent testing

---

## Employee Coupon Code

Use:

```text
SUPEREMPLOYEE1MG
```

For:

- Employee health assessment booking

---

## Dependent Coupon Code

Use:

```text
SUPERDEP1MG
```

For:

- Immediate family member bookings

---

## Important Booking Rule

- Employee coupon code must not be used for dependents.
- Dependent coupon code must not be used for employees.

Incorrect usage may result in ineligibility for benefits.

---

# Health Reports

## Report Availability

Test reports are generally available:

- Within 48 hours after sample collection

---

## Additional Benefits

Once reports are generated, employees receive coupon codes for:

### Complimentary Consultations

- 1 Free General Physician Consultation
- 1 Free Dietician Consultation

These consultations are provided through Tata 1MG.

---

# Booking Process

## Step 1: Register or Log In

Employee must:

- Create a Tata 1MG account, or
- Log in to an existing account

---

## Step 2: Add Official Work Email

To access organizational benefits:

- Official work email must be added to the Tata 1MG profile
- Work email verification is mandatory

Without work-email validation:

- Corporate health benefits may not be available

---

## Step 3: Access Corporate Dashboard

After verification:

- Open Corporate Dashboard within Tata 1MG
- Access available health assessment packages

---

## Step 4: Book Assessment

Employee should:

1. Select the health assessment package
2. Choose preferred laboratory
3. Select available slot
4. Provide patient details
5. Confirm booking

---

## Step 5: Health Risk Assessment

Employees may additionally complete:

- Health Risk Assessment

This helps generate:

- Personalized health insights
- Smart health reports

---

# Key Rules Summary

- Available to eligible on-roll employees only.
- Complimentary annual health checkup.
- Immediate family members (up to 4) eligible at subsidized rates.
- Includes approximately 67 tests across 11 health profiles.
- Delivered through Tata 1MG.
- Home and office sample collection available.
- Appointment booking available on weekdays and weekends.
- Work email verification is required to access corporate benefits.
- Health reports are generally available within 48 hours of sample collection.
- Free physician and dietician consultations are available after report generation.
- Employee and dependent coupon codes must be used correctly.
