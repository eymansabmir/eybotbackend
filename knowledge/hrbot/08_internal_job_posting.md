# DiversifEY Internal Job Posting Policy

## Purpose

DiversifEY is the organization's internal job posting program that enables employees to explore career opportunities outside their current role based on their skills, experience, and business requirements.

The program supports:

- Career agility
- Internal mobility
- Cross-service-line exposure
- Professional growth and development

---

# Eligibility

Employees may apply for Internal Job Postings (IJP) if they satisfy all applicable eligibility criteria at the time of application.

## Eligible Employees

Employees must:

- Be on the rolls of the organization.
- Have completed at least 18 months in their current role.
- Not be on an active Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).
- Not be subject to active disciplinary proceedings.
- Not have received a warning letter within the previous 12 months.

---

## Special Eligibility

### Articleship Employees

Employees may apply if they:

- Have completed 2 years of articleship with the organization.
- Are currently at:
  - Intern 4
  - CSS
  - Associate Consultant
  - Executive

### Employees on Bench

Employees on bench may apply:

- Regardless of tenure with the organization.

---

## NTP / PIP Rules

Employees who received a:

- Need to Progress (NTP)

rating in the previous performance cycle are eligible only after:

- Successful completion of the associated PIP.

---

## Ineligible Employees

The following categories are not eligible:

- Fixed Term Employees (FTEs)
- Interns
- Retainers
- External Contractors

---

# Application Limits

An employee may maintain:

- Maximum 5 active IJP applications at one time

Additional applications can only be submitted after closure of an existing application.

---

# Cooling Period

Employees who decline an IJP offer:

- Cannot apply for another IJP for 6 months from the date of offer decline.

---

# Employee Declaration

At the time of application employees must:

- Confirm they have read the policy.
- Self-declare compliance with eligibility criteria.

Providing false or misleading information may result in:

- Application cancellation
- Disciplinary action

---

# Selection Disclaimer

Participation in the IJP process does not guarantee selection.

Selection decisions are based on:

- Business priorities
- Role fitment
- Assessment outcomes
- Interview performance

---

# Internal Job Posting Process

## Step 1: Application

Employees should:

1. Review opportunities available on the DiversifEY Portal.
2. Discuss career interests with their Counselor.
3. Submit an application for relevant positions.

Required uploads:

- Updated Resume
- LEAD Year-End Dashboard for the previous 2 performance years
- Mandatory self-declaration

---

## Step 2: Eligibility Review

Home Service Line HR reviews:

- Eligibility criteria
- Compliance requirements

Target timeline:

- Within 3 working days

---

## Step 3: Reporting Partner Approval

Applications meeting eligibility requirements are routed to:

- CFTL
- Reporting Partner

Approval timeline:

- Within 3 working days

If no action is taken:

- Approval is considered automatically granted
- Application proceeds to assessment stage

Applications that do not meet eligibility requirements are rejected and updated in the portal.

---

## Step 4: Assessment

The Talent Acquisition (TA) team coordinates:

- Interviews
- Assessments
- Hiring Manager interactions

Employees receive status updates through:

- DiversifEY Portal

---

## Step 5: Fitment & Offer

Following successful assessment:

- Host Service Line HR
- Talent Acquisition Team
- Hiring Manager / Partner

finalize:

- Level fitment
- Compensation
- Movement timelines

Offer details are shared through:

- DiversifEY Portal
- Email

---

## Step 6: Offer Acceptance

Employees must:

- Accept or decline the offer within 3 working days

Failure to accept within timeline may impact processing.

---

## Step 7: Movement

Upon acceptance:

- Home Service Line HR initiates transfer activities.
- Effective movement date is confirmed.

Application status becomes:

- IJP Completed

All other active IJP applications are automatically closed.

---

# Movement Guidelines

## Transition Timeline

Employees should move to the new team within:

- 2 months of offer acceptance

subject to:

- Knowledge transfer
- Handover completion
