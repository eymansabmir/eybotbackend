# Financial Support for Medical Exigencies

## Purpose

This policy provides financial assistance to employees facing significant medical emergencies affecting themselves or their eligible dependents, after the organization's mediclaim coverage has been fully exhausted.

---

# Scope

The organization may provide financial support for medical expenses ranging from:

- INR 50,000 to INR 3,00,000

This support is:

- In addition to the employee's mediclaim hospitalization entitlement.
- Available only after the insured amount under the mediclaim policy has been exhausted.

---

# Eligibility

## Eligible Employees

This policy applies to:

- Employees on the rolls of the organization
- Employees at Senior Consultant / Assistant Manager level and below

---

## Eligible Dependents

Dependents include:

- Family members declared under the organization's medical insurance policy

---

## Important Note

Financial assistance under this policy:

- Is discretionary
- Is not an employee entitlement
- May be approved or declined based on management review

---

# Eligibility Conditions

Employees may apply only when:

- The mediclaim coverage has been fully exhausted.
- The medical expense requirement is at least INR 50,000.

Requests below INR 50,000 are not eligible.

---

# Required Documentation

Employees must submit:

- Medical reports
- Hospital documents
- Medical bills
- Expense estimates
- Any additional supporting documentation

Documentation must originate from:

- Accredited hospitals or healthcare providers

---

# Financial Assistance Structure

The assistance is provided in the form of a loan.

## Loan Amount

- Minimum: INR 50,000
- Maximum: INR 3,00,000

---

## Loan Tenure

Maximum repayment tenure:

- 24 months

Tenure is calculated from:

- Date of first installment

---

## Interest Rate

### First Year

- 0% interest

### After First Year

Any outstanding balance remaining after completion of the first year will attract:

- 9% annual interest

---

# Bonus Adjustment Rule

During the loan tenure:

- Any performance bonus
- Any additional bonus

will automatically be adjusted against the outstanding loan balance.

Adjustment amount:

- Up to 100% of bonus
- Limited to the remaining loan balance

---

# Prepayment Option

Employees may repay the loan:

- Fully, or
- Partially

at any time before the scheduled repayment completion date.

---

## Prepayment Process

For full or partial loan repayment:

1. Transfer the repayment amount to the organization's designated bank account.
2. Notify the HR Payroll team.
3. Repayment details will be updated in organizational records.

---

# Approval Process

## Step 1: Request Submission

Employee submits request to:

- Service Line HR (SLHR)

Required documents:

- Medical reports
- Bills
- Expense estimates
- Supporting evidence of the medical exigency

---

## Step 2: HR Review & Approval

The request requires:

- Approval from the respective SL HR Lead

After approval:

- Documentation and approvals are forwarded to the Talent Enablement Leader.

---

## Step 3: Exception Cases

Any exceptions to policy requirements require approval from:

- National Talent Leader

---

## Step 4: Loan Processing

Once required approvals are obtained:

- Loan will be processed within 3 working days.

---

## Step 5: Loan Communication

The employee will receive:

- Loan approval details
- Disbursement information
- Repayment schedule
- Loan terms and conditions

via official HR Payroll communication.

The employee must acknowledge the communication within the prescribed turnaround time (TAT).

If no response is received within the prescribed period:

- The terms will be treated as accepted by the employee.

---

# Key Rules Summary

- Applicable to employees at Senior Consultant / Assistant Manager level and below.
- Dependents covered must be registered under the organization's medical insurance policy.
- Mediclaim coverage must be exhausted before requesting assistance.
- Minimum eligible request amount: INR 50,000.
- Maximum support amount: INR 3,00,000.
- Support is provided as a loan.
- Maximum loan tenure: 24 months.
- No interest during first year.
- 9% interest applies on outstanding balance after first year.
- Employees may prepay partially or fully at any time.
- Performance and additional bonuses are adjusted against loan balance during repayment.
- Approval is discretionary and subject to management review.
- Loan processing occurs within 3 working days after final approval.
