# Compensation Policy

## Purpose

The compensation framework is designed to:

- Reward performance and contribution.
- Maintain internal pay equity.
- Remain competitive with market standards.
- Support long-term business sustainability.

Key compensation principles:

- Pay for performance
- Internal equity
- Market competitiveness
- Financial responsibility

---

# Eligibility

Applicable to:

- Permanent employees
- Fixed Term Employees (FTEs)

---

# Compensation Structure

Employee compensation consists of:

## Cost to Company (CTC)

CTC may include:

### Fixed Compensation

- Basic Salary
- House Rent Allowance (HRA)
- Conveyance Allowance
- Special Allowance
- Statutory Bonus (where applicable)

### Cash Perquisites

Examples:

- Leave Travel Allowance (LTA)
- Telephone Reimbursement

### Other Perquisites

Examples:

- Company Car Lease
- House Lease / Lease Deposit
- Provident Fund
- National Pension System (NPS)
- Critical Illness Coverage
- Medical Insurance Top-Up

---

## Annual Performance Bonus

Purpose:

- Reward individual performance.
- Drive business-aligned behaviors.
- Share organization success with employees.

General payout timing:

- Annually (typically September unless otherwise communicated)

### CIS Applicability

For Senior Managers and above:

- Annual bonus payout may be subject to Consolidated Inventory Status (CIS) requirements.

---

# Annual CTC Revision & Bonus Eligibility

Employees are eligible if:

- Joined on or before 31 March of the applicable performance year.
- Remain on the organization's rolls at the time of communication and payout.
- Are not serving notice period at communication and payout date.
