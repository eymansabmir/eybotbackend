# Leave Policy

## Purpose

The Leave Policy provides employees with structured leave benefits to support personal wellbeing, family responsibilities, health needs, and work-life balance.

---

# Eligibility

Applicable to:

- All on-roll employees
- Fixed-Term Employees (FTEs)

Not applicable to:

- Interns
- Trainees
- Contractors

---

# Annual Leave

Annual Leave is managed for the period:

- 1 July to 30 June

Annual Leave includes:

- Earned Leave
- Casual Leave
- Sick / Wellbeing Leave

---

## General Annual Leave Rules

### Leave Credit

Leave is credited:

- At the beginning of the leave year, or
- At joining, whichever is later

### Leave Calculation

Only working days are counted as leave.

The following are excluded:

- Saturdays
- Sundays
- Public holidays

---

## Leave Application Process

- Earned Leave should be applied at least 2 weeks in advance.
- Leave requests must be submitted through the Leave Management System (LMS).
- Reporting Manager approval is required.
- Employees must ensure approved leave is reflected in timesheets.

---

# Earned Leave

## Entitlement

- 15 working days per year
- Pro-rated for partial years of service

Employees are encouraged to take:

- At least 10 days of leave annually for rest and rejuvenation

---

## Carry Forward

Unused Earned Leave may be carried forward.

Maximum carry-forward limit:

- 45 days

---

## Encashment

Earned Leave can be encashed:

- Only upon separation from the organization

Maximum encashment:

- 30 days

Conditions:

- Full notice period must be served

---

## Notice Period Adjustment

During notice period:

- Only Earned Leave can be used.

If notice period is not fully served:

- Unserved notice period is adjusted against Earned Leave balance.
- Adjustment is calculated using calendar days.

---

## Leave Encashment Formula

```text
(Annual Basic Salary ÷ 365) × Number of Earned Leave Days
```

Encashment is:

- Subject to applicable taxes

---

# Casual Leave

## Entitlement

- 7 working days per year
- Pro-rated for partial years of service

Purpose:

- Personal and ad-hoc requirements

---

## Usage Rules

Normally:

- Can be taken only one day at a time

The following are treated as Earned Leave:

- More than one consecutive day
- Friday and following Monday combination
- Clubbing with public holidays

### Exception

If Earned Leave balance is exhausted:

- Casual Leave may be availed for multiple days.

---

## Carry Forward

Not permitted.

Unused Casual Leave:

- Automatically lapses at year-end.

---

## Encashment

Not permitted.

---

# Sick / Wellbeing Leave

## Entitlement

- 5 working days per year
- Pro-rated for partial years of service

---

## Permitted Usage

May be used for:

- Personal illness
- Mental wellbeing
- Caregiving responsibilities
- Childcare support
- Health assessments
- Personal wellbeing activities

---

## Extended Medical Absence

Employees requiring longer medical leave should:

- Contact Service Line HR

---

## Carry Forward

Not permitted.

Unused Sick / Wellbeing Leave:

- Automatically lapses

---

## Encashment

Not permitted.

---

# Other Leave Information

## Leave Balance

Employees can view leave balances through:

- Leave Management System (LMS)

---

## Clubbing of Leave

Where extended leave is required:

- Casual Leave may be combined with Earned Leave.

---

## Negative Leave Balance

If leave exceeds available entitlement:

- Excess leave is treated as Leave Without Pay (LOP)
- Salary deductions will apply

Calculation includes:

- Excess leave taken
- Loss of future leave accrual

---

## Sabbatical Leave

Employees taking sabbatical leave are governed by:

- Sabbatical Leave Policy

---

# Parental Support Leave

Includes:

- Maternity Leave
- Adoption Leave
- Commissioning Parent Leave
- Paternity Leave
- Surviving Parent Leave
- Tubectomy Leave
- Parental Benefit

---

# Maternity Leave

## Eligibility

Women employees who have worked:

- At least 80 days in the previous 12 months before expected delivery

---

## Entitlement

Maximum:

- 26 weeks paid leave

Condition:

- Not more than 8 weeks before expected delivery

---

## Additional Leave

Employees may combine Maternity Leave with:

- Earned Leave
- Casual Leave
- Sick / Wellbeing Leave

---

## Miscarriage / Medical Termination of Pregnancy (MTP)

Entitlement:

- Up to 6 weeks paid leave

Requirement:

- Medical certification

---

# Adoption Leave

## Child Up To 1 Year Old

Entitlement:

- 26 weeks paid leave

---

## Child Above 1 Year and Up To 5 Years

Entitlement:

- 12 weeks paid leave

---

## Eligibility

Applicable to:

- Woman employees
- Single parents

---

# Commissioning Parent Leave

Applicable to:

- Woman employees
- Single parents using surrogacy

Entitlement:

- 26 weeks paid leave

Starting from:

- Date child is handed over

---

# Paternity Leave

## Eligibility

All male employees.

## Entitlement

- 20 working days

Must be used within:

- 7 months of childbirth
- Adoption
- Commissioning

Unused entitlement:

- Lapses after 7 months

---

# Surviving Parent Leave

If the mother passes away within:

- First 6 months of childbirth, adoption, or commissioning

The surviving parent may receive:

- Up to 26 weeks leave

---

# Tubectomy Leave

Applicable to:

- Women employees undergoing tubectomy

Entitlement:

- 2 weeks paid leave

Immediately following the procedure.

---

# Parental Benefit

The organization provides:

- INR 7,500 parental benefit

Applicable to:

- New mothers
- New fathers
- Adoptive parents
- Commissioning parents

---

# Special Leave

## Bereavement Leave

### Entitlement

- 5 working days

Must be used within:

- 60 days of the death

---

### Immediate Family Definition

Includes:

- Spouse
- Parents
- Parents-in-law
- Grandparents
- Siblings
- Children

Any additional absence is adjusted against available leave balances.

---

# Block Leave

The organization may declare:

- Year-end block leave

Purpose:

- Encourage employees to disconnect, rest, and rejuvenate

Block leave may consist of:

- Employer-contributed leave
- Earned Leave

---

## Business Exception

If block leave cannot be utilized because of business needs:

- Firm-sponsored leave may be credited

Validity:

- Until 31 March of the following year

Unused balance lapses thereafter.

---

# Shared Leave Program

## Purpose

Allows employees to donate Earned Leave to colleagues facing personal hardships.

---

## Donation Rules

- Maximum donation to one colleague:
  - 5 days

- Recipient can receive:
  - Up to 10 days per year

---

## Eligibility Conditions

Shared Leave can only be used when:

- Earned Leave is exhausted
- Casual Leave is exhausted

---

## Donation Restrictions

Allowed:

- Peer to peer
- Senior to junior

Not allowed:

- Junior to senior

---

## Notice Period Restriction

Employees serving notice period:

- Cannot donate leave
- Cannot receive leave

---

## Carry Forward & Encashment

Shared Leave:

- Cannot be carried forward
- Cannot be encashed

Effective from:

- 1 April 2025

---

# Leave Record Compliance

Employees must:

- Apply leave in LMS
- Record leave in timesheets

---

## Leave Mismatch Process

Where LMS and timesheet records differ:

- Employee receives notification
- 21 days provided to correct records

If unresolved:

- Considered policy violation
- May lead to disciplinary action

---

# Public Holidays

Annual entitlement:

- 11 public holidays

Includes:

- 9 fixed holidays
- 2 flexible holidays

---

## Eligibility

Applicable to:

- Employees
- Interns
- Trainees
- Contractors

---

## Client Deployments

Employees working full-time at client sites may be required to follow:

- Client holiday calendar

---

## Flexible Holidays

Employees may select flexible holidays from:

- Approved organization holiday list

Selection is independent of office location.
