# Equal Opportunity Policy

## Purpose

The organization is committed to fostering an inclusive, diverse, and equitable workplace by ensuring equal employment opportunities for all individuals, irrespective of:

- Race
- Religion
- Belief
- Sex
- Color
- Creed
- Age
- National origin
- Ethnic origin
- Marital status
- Pregnancy
- Sexual orientation
- Economic background
- Physical ability

The organization is committed to maintaining fair, transparent, and merit-based employment practices.

---

# Applicability

This policy applies to:

- Existing employees
- Job applicants
- Interns
- Trainees
- Fixed-Term Employees (FTEs)
- Contractual staff
- Personnel covered under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPwD) Act, 2016

The policy applies regardless of:

- Rank
- Level
- Employment status
- Probation status

---

# Equal Opportunity Commitment

The organization is an equal opportunity employer and is committed to:

- Providing an inclusive workplace
- Treating all personnel with dignity and respect
- Preventing discrimination in any aspect of employment
- Creating an environment where persons with disabilities can perform and succeed

---

# Non-Discrimination Policy

Discrimination is prohibited in all employment-related matters, including:

- Recruitment
- Hiring
- Transfers
- Promotions
- Compensation
- Career development
- Assignment of work
- Separation from employment
- Workplace conditions
- Location-based decisions
- Benefits and privileges

All employment decisions are based solely on:

- Skills
- Qualifications
- Competence
- Performance

---

# Zero Tolerance Policy

The organization maintains zero tolerance towards:

- Bullying
- Harassment
- Discrimination
- Unfair treatment

Any behavior inconsistent with these principles may be subject to corrective or disciplinary action under applicable policies.

---

# Accessibility Commitment

The organization is committed to creating an accessible and barrier-free workplace in accordance with the RPwD Act.

Accessibility measures may include:

- Access to workplace facilities
- Access to common amenities
- Access to information and technology
- Assistive devices where feasible
- Workplace modifications that enable employees to perform their duties effectively

Infrastructure improvements and accommodations will be implemented wherever reasonably practical and feasible.

---

# Reasonable Accommodation

The organization is committed to providing reasonable assistance and accommodations that enable persons with disabilities to:

- Perform their job duties effectively
- Participate in training programs
- Attend meetings and events
- Advance in their careers
- Access workplace facilities and services

Reasonable accommodation may include necessary modifications or adjustments to support effective participation at work.

---

# Requesting Accommodation

Employees requiring workplace accommodations or accessibility support may request assistance through:

- Service Line Talent Team
- Location Talent Team
- Purple Champions Support

### Accessibility Requests May Include

Examples include:

- Special requirements during hiring
- Onboarding accommodations
- Office accessibility requirements
- Access to information and technology
- Meeting accommodations
- Event and offsite accommodations
- Workplace adjustments

Medical documentation may be requested where necessary to support the accommodation request.

---

# Employees with Disabilities

The organization seeks to provide a supportive environment for employees with disabilities and ensure fair treatment in all employment matters.

---

## Change of Location

As a general principle:

- Employees with disabilities are normally exempt from location transfers, subject to business requirements.

### Where Transfer Becomes Necessary

If relocation becomes unavoidable due to promotion or business requirements:

- The employee should be placed as close as practical to their original location.
- Transfers to remote or far-off locations should be avoided wherever possible.

---

# Disability Declaration and Records

In accordance with the RPwD Act, the organization may maintain records relating to employees who voluntarily disclose disabilities.

Examples may include:

- Date of joining
- Contact information
- Nature of disability
- Other information required for compliance and accommodation purposes

---

## Voluntary Disclosure

Employees who wish to be covered under disability-related support provisions may:

- Contact their Service Line Talent Team
- Contact their Location Talent Team
- Submit a request through the designated accessibility support channel

Disclosure of disability:

- Is entirely voluntary
- Will be treated confidentially

---

# Training and Career Development

The organization is committed to ensuring that persons with disabilities receive:

- Equal access to learning opportunities
- Participation in training programs
- Career development opportunities
- Support required for professional advancement

---

# Reporting Concerns

Employees are encouraged to raise concerns regarding:

- Discrimination
- Harassment
- Accessibility barriers
- Unequal treatment
- Accommodation needs

The organization provides grievance and listening mechanisms for reporting such concerns and seeking appropriate support.

---

# Key Rules Summary

- Equal opportunity applies to all employment-related decisions.
- Discrimination on protected grounds is prohibited.
- Bullying, harassment, and discrimination are subject to zero tolerance.
- Reasonable accommodations are available for persons with disabilities.
- Accessibility requests may cover recruitment, onboarding, workplace access, meetings, and events.
- Disability disclosure is voluntary and confidential.
- Employees with disabilities are generally exempt from location transfers unless business requirements necessitate otherwise.
- Career development and training opportunities should remain accessible and inclusive.
