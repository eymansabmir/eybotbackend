# Day Care Policy

## Purpose

The Day Care Policy supports working mothers and single parents by providing access to childcare facilities and reimbursement support, helping employees balance professional and parental responsibilities.

---

# Eligibility

Eligible employees:

- Working mothers on the rolls of the organization
- Single parents on the rolls of the organization

### Karnataka Exception

Effective 1 November 2024:

- Both working parents are eligible in Karnataka.

### Child Eligibility

Eligible children must be:

- Between 6 months and 6 years of age

### Maximum Coverage

- Up to 2 children per employee are eligible for reimbursement.

---

# Approved Day Care Facilities

The organization has partnered with:

- KLAY
- Medhaam

### KLAY

- Multiple locations across India
- Operating hours:
  - Monday to Friday
  - 8:30 AM to 7:30 PM
- Employees may choose any continuous 9-hour window.

### Gurugram Facility

In-house facility at:

- Sector 44, Gurugram

Managed by:

- Medhaam

Operating hours:

- 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM
- All working days

### Alternate Day Care Centers

Employees may use a daycare center other than KLAY or Medhaam if:

- Approved centers are not convenient due to distance

Reimbursement remains available within policy limits.

---

# Enrollment & Registration

To enroll:

1. Complete registration with the daycare provider.
2. Sign the enrollment form.

### Minimum Commitment

#### KLAY

- Minimum commitment period: 3 months

#### Medhaam (Sector 44)

- No mandatory 3-month commitment
- Usage can be customized in advance

### Cancellation

Employees wishing to discontinue must:

- Provide at least 1 month's written notice to the daycare provider

### Capacity Restrictions

Where daycare capacity is limited:

- Waiting lists are maintained
- Allocation follows first-come, first-served basis

---

# Reimbursement

## Reimbursement Amount

Employees may claim:

- 50% of monthly daycare fees
- Maximum reimbursement:
  - INR 10,000 per month per child

Reimbursement is paid as:

- Ex-gratia payment
- Credited with the following month's salary

---

## Gurugram Medhaam Exception

For Sector 44 Medhaam facility:

- Employee pays 50% directly to Medhaam
- Organization pays remaining 50%

No reimbursement claim process is required.

---

## Backdated Claims

### KLAY

- Maximum reimbursement period:
  - Last 3 months only

### Other Day Care Centers

- Maximum reimbursement period:
  - Last 3 months only

Claims older than 3 months are not eligible.

---

# Expenses Eligible for Reimbursement

Eligible:

- Monthly daycare fees

Not eligible:

- Admission fee
- Food charges
- App fees
- Form fees
- CCTV fees
- Preschool fees
- Nursery fees
- Tuition-related services
- Any additional daycare services

---

# Preschool / Nursery Clarification

Where daycare and preschool services are combined:

- Invoice must separately show:
  - Daycare charges
  - Preschool charges

Only daycare charges are reimbursable.

---

# Employees on Sabbatical

Employees on sabbatical:

- Continue to be eligible for daycare reimbursement.

---

# Required Documents

Claims must include:

## Mandatory

- Child Birth Certificate
- Invoice
- Receipt (if issued)
- Proof of Payment

### Invoice Requirements

Invoice must clearly mention:

- Service period/month
- Day Care services

If daycare and preschool are combined:

- Charges must be separately itemized

---

## Payment Proof Requirements

Payment proof must show:

- Employee bank account
- Daycare bank account
- Transaction details

---

# Payment Conditions

## Accepted Payment Method

Only:

- Online payments

are eligible for reimbursement.

### Payment Source Requirement

Payment must be made by:

- Eligible employee

Spouse payment is accepted only when:

- Both parents are employees of the organization.

---

# GST Requirements

## If Daycare is GST Registered

Invoice must include:

- GSTIN
- GST breakup

Reimbursement applies only to:

- Base fee

GST amount is not reimbursable.

---

## If GST Is Not Charged

Employees must provide:

- Signed and stamped declaration from daycare

confirming:

- GST registration status
- GST not charged

---

# Special Documentation Situations

Additional signed and stamped daycare declaration may be required when:

- Invoices are not issued
- Payments were made to a different account
- Fees are labeled as:
  - Kids Learning Fee
  - Tuition Fee
  - Similar terminology

The declaration must confirm that charges relate to daycare services.

---

# Claim Submission Guidelines

Employees should:

- Submit all supporting documents together
- Preferably upload a single ZIP file or consolidated document package

Additional documentation may be requested by Finance at any stage.

---

# Recovery of Reimbursement

Reimbursed amounts may be recovered if:

- Employee's Last Working Day (LWD) falls on or before the 15th of the month
- Incorrect or inconsistent information is discovered
- Documents fail verification requirements

---

# Health & Safety

Employees utilizing daycare facilities must:

- Follow all health and safety requirements established by the daycare provider.

---

# Cost Structure (Reference Information)

### Admission Fee

- INR 15,000 standard admission fee
- 100% waived under the corporate arrangement

### Typical Monthly Daycare Fee Range

Depending on location:

- Approximately INR 15,000 to INR 32,500 per month

Additional charges such as:

- Food fees
- CCTV fees
- App fees
- Form fees

are generally employee-borne and not reimbursable.

---

# Important Conditions

- Reimbursement is restricted to daycare services only.
- Maximum reimbursement is INR 10,000 per month per child.
- Maximum 2 children can be covered.
- Claims must be supported with valid documentation.
- Only online payments are accepted.
- Employees on sabbatical remain eligible.
- Backdated reimbursement claims are limited to 3 months.
- Daycare providers operate independently and service delivery responsibility remains with the provider.
